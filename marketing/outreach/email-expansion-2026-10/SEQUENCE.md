# Email sequence for the 100+ estate expansion

Status: Draft for owner approval before the first batch loads
Updated: 2026-09-27 | Owner: Kaushik Majumder

The September sequence led with face attendance. Every competitor says the same thing, and the campaign got no recorded replies. This sequence leads with what the owner can check and show: hazira, wage and PF records. It is built for a reader who does not know us and does not trust email from strangers.

Rules that apply to every email:

- Plain text. No attachment, no image, no calendar link, no tracking pixel.
- Use `{{first_name}}` and `{{company_name}}` only when verified. Never invent a personal detail.
- One question per email. Easy stop line.
- No client names, no savings figures, no "stops", no accusations about managers.
- The purpose of the email is a phone call or a meeting, not a sale.

## Reply handling

- Any reply, even a question: call or reply within one working day. Offer the short call or a visit.
- "We already have software": "Understood. What does it cover today: hazira, weighing, payroll? If it all works well, I will leave it there. If there is a gap, I can tell you honestly whether we can fill it."
- "Send details": send the one-page sample report and the website link. No brochure bundle.
- "Who uses it?": give region-level experience. Share a name only if a customer agreed to be a private reference.
- Stop or not interested: suppress at once, across email, phone and WhatsApp.
