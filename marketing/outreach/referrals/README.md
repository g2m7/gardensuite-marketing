# GardenSuite referral program

Status: CURRENT (under [CURRENT_STRATEGY.md](../CURRENT_STRATEGY.md))
Updated: 2026-09-27 | Owner: Kaushik Majumder, Sarbani Associates

Referrals already close faster than any other channel. The owner has approved cash commission in principle. The structure and the amounts below are options for the owner to choose. Until the owner picks one, quote no commission figure to any referrer.

## Who can refer

| Referrer | Why they matter | How to find them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer owners and directors | Their word carries the most weight with peers | Ask during a support visit or review meeting, once their system is running well |
| Managers who have used GardenSuite and moved to another estate | Managers move between gardens often. A manager who used it knows it works. | Keep a list of past and present users at customer gardens. Note where each one moves. |
| PF and ESI consultants | They see which gardens have record problems | Siliguri, Jalpaiguri, Alipurduar, Dibrugarh, Tinsukia, Jorhat |
| CA firms and statutory auditors serving estates | Trusted by owners on compliance and cost | Ask customers who audits them |
| Visiting agents and tea consultants | Trusted on garden practice | Associations, customer introductions |
| Weighing scale, electronics and computer dealers | Already sell to gardens | Siliguri, Jalpaiguri and Upper Assam markets |
| Garden suppliers (fertiliser, chemicals, machinery) | Visit gardens every month | Ask customers for their supplier names |

## Commission options

All options pay only after the referred estate signs and its first payment reaches Sarbani Associates. The referrer must have made the introduction before the estate was already in active talks with us. Hardware, travel and taxes are left out of the contract value.

### Option A: 10% of first-year contract value

- Pros:
  - Grows with deal size, so bigger introductions earn more.
  - Easy to explain: "one-tenth of what they pay us in year one".
- Cons:
  - The payout is unclear until the quote is final, and low-trust referrers want a known number.
  - A large ERP deal gives a large payout, which may feel out of proportion.
  - We must define "contract value" in writing.

### Option B: Flat fee by estate size (recommended to start)

Example bands (proposal only, owner to set):

| Referred account | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Bought-leaf factory or estate under 300 workers | Rs 25,000 |
| Estate with 300 to 800 workers | Rs 40,000 |
| Estate with over 800 workers, or a group of 2+ gardens | Rs 60,000 |

- Pros:
  - The simplest option. The referrer knows the exact amount before making the call, and that builds trust.
  - Predictable cost for us.
  - Easy to pay and record.
- Cons:
  - Overpays on a small package deal and underpays on a full ERP deal.
  - Gives no reason to help after the sale.

### Option C: 5% of first year, plus 2% of AMC for the next 2 years

- Pros:
  - Keeps the referrer interested in the customer staying happy.
  - Builds a long relationship with consultants and dealers.
  - Spreads our cost over time.
- Cons:
  - The hardest option to track and explain.
  - Referrers wait a long time for most of the money.
  - Unattractive in a market where people prefer cash now.

### Recommendation

Start with **Option B** for the first six months. Low-trust referrers respond best to a clear number paid fast.

- Pay within 7 days of the referred estate's first payment.
- Add a one-time bonus of Rs 10,000 when a referred estate later adds a full ERP module.
- Review after six months or ten referred deals, whichever comes first. Switch to Option A or C only if deal sizes vary widely.

## Payment rules

- Use a one-page written referral agreement. It covers the referrer's name, the estate referred, the date, the option and amount, and the payment trigger.
- Pay by bank transfer, with an invoice or receipt. Deduct TDS as the CA advises (commission falls under Section 194H).
- Do not pay:
  - the buying estate's own employees, unless the buying owner agrees in writing
  - government officials, task force members or union office-bearers, in any form
- A referral never reopens a Do Not Contact account.
- Record every referral in the tracker: referrer, estate, date, permission to use the referrer's name, next action, outcome and payout.

## How to ask

To a customer, after a good support visit or review:

> Is there one garden owner you know who is still doing hazira and wages on paper or old software? If you are comfortable, could you ask them if they would take a call from me? If they buy, we pay a referral fee of Rs [amount] as a thank-you.

To a consultant, CA firm or dealer:

> We make tea garden software at Sarbani Associates, Bagdogra, and have worked with tea estates since 2000. When one of your gardens has trouble with hazira, wage or PF records, you can introduce us. If the garden buys, we pay you Rs [amount], agreed in writing.

A message the referrer can forward:

> I know Kaushik from Sarbani Associates, Bagdogra. They make GardenSuite, which records hazira, leaf weight and wages for tea gardens, and it works without internet in the field. Would you like to see it? I can share your number with him.

Let the referrer describe their own experience. Never write a testimonial in their voice.

## Host gardens

A prospect seeing GardenSuite running at a real garden is the strongest proof we have.

- Choose one or two customer gardens in North Bengal, and one in Assam if possible, where the system runs well.
- Ask the owner for written permission. Record what visitors may see, which days suit, and whether the garden's name can be told to the visitor.
- Thank the host for each visit. Examples: a free support visit, an AMC discount or a small annual thank-you. The owner decides.
- Visitors see only the attendance and weighing process, never worker personal data or wage figures.
- If no host is available, record a walkthrough video at a customer garden, with the owner's written permission and no worker faces or names shown.

## Targets for the first 60 days

- 10 referral partners who have agreed in writing to introduce (customers, consultants, dealers).
- 10 referral asks to customers, with the answer recorded: yes, no or later.
- 1 host garden with written permission.
- 5 introduced estate conversations.
- 2 demos or site visits from referrals.
