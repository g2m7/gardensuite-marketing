# Proposed After-Sales Response Policy

Status: INTERNAL PROPOSAL - OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED

This is not a current service guarantee. Do not place the targets below in an email, quotation, brochure, website, or agreement until Sarbani Associates confirms that the team can meet them.

## Documented support facts

The current repository supports these statements:

- GardenSuite is built and supported by Sarbani Associates in Bagdogra, Siliguri.
- Regular assistance may be provided by phone, WhatsApp, email, video call, and remote desktop.
- Requests are handled according to severity and business impact.
- Critical problems affecting payroll, attendance, data access, or core operations receive the highest priority for remote diagnosis and escalation.
- Setup and rollout time depends on modules, devices, worker records, data work, infrastructure readiness, staff availability, location, and agreed scope.
- Any on-site work must be confirmed according to the applicable quotation, travel requirements, team availability, and location.

The current repository does not contain an approved customer-facing response or resolution SLA.

## Recommended support flow

1. The client raises an issue through an approved phone, WhatsApp, email, video, or remote-support channel.
2. Sarbani Associates records the estate, affected module, business impact, contact person, and time reported.
3. The team assigns a severity and acknowledges the request.
4. Remote diagnosis begins first whenever practical.
5. The client receives the likely cause, next action, and expected resolution or update time.
6. If physical work is required, the visit scope, cost, travel, and timing are confirmed in writing.
7. The issue is closed only after the client is informed and the outcome is recorded.

## Recommended service targets

These targets separate first response from resolution. A first response means that the issue has been acknowledged and triage has begun. It does not mean the issue has been fixed.

| Severity | Example | Proposed first response | Proposed resolution target |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Critical | Attendance, payroll, data access, or a core operation is unavailable | Within 4 business hours | Within 1 business day when the cause is within GardenSuite software and required access is available |
| High | A major function is unavailable but some work can continue | Within 1 business day | Within 2 business days when the cause is within GardenSuite software |
| Normal | A limited issue with a practical workaround | Within 2 business days | Within 3 to 5 business days |
| Request | Report change, configuration change, training request, or new requirement | Within 3 business days | Timeline provided after assessment |

Recommended business hours for owner review: Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM India Standard Time, excluding published holidays.

## Conditions that can change resolution time

- Power, internet, mobile network, or third-party service failure
- Unsupported or damaged client hardware
- Physical scale, device, cable, or local network work
- Missing login access, records, backups, screenshots, or staff availability
- Data correction or recovery where the size and condition are not yet known
- A new feature, report, integration, or custom change
- Travel or on-site work

For these cases, Sarbani Associates should still meet the approved first-response target and provide the next update time, but should not promise the standard resolution target.
