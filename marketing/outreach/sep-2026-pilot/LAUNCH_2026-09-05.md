## Rules for adding another estate

An estate can enter this live campaign only when all of the following are recorded in the canonical tracker:

1. It is in Dibrugarh or Tinsukia and passes the pilot account rules.
2. It is not a current client, suppressed account, public company, large multi-estate group, previously rejected account without new evidence, or active sales discussion.
3. The email is publicly associated with the estate or its managing company.
4. A named owner, director, general manager, estate manager or garden manager is recorded.
5. Snov reports the address as `Valid`.
6. `suppressed` is `No` and `owner_approved` is `Yes` in `prospects.csv`.

Add at most four newly cleared estates on a working day. The mailbox cap counts first emails and follow-ups together. Do not add a new batch when replies, provider warnings or bounce review require attention.
