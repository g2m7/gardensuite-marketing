# Pilot Research - 7 September 2026

This pass checked the legacy Excel tracker before public research. No new account was added to `prospects.csv`.

## Screened out

- Bijlibari Tea Estate: the legacy tracker records negotiation in progress and a pending call. Excluded as an active sales discussion.
- Mahabirbari Tea Estate: public records indicate suitable scale, but current company and group sources show several tea operations. Excluded for group fit.
- Hatimara Tea Estate: the operator reports 271.13 hectares and 273 permanent employees. It sits within a three-estate tea business and a larger connected company network. Excluded for group fit.

## Deferred

- Helenbari Tea Estate: current operating evidence and a 318-account scale proxy were found. The only named owner evidence found was from 2018, so the decision-maker link is too old.
- Mulchan Bag Tea Estate: a 227-account scale proxy and 75.21-hectare historical area were found. No current owner or qualifying manager was found.
- Sadhna Tea Garden: current Dibrugarh operating evidence and a 155-account scale proxy were found. No current owner or qualifying manager was found.
- Sookerating Tea Estate: a published business address was found in an estate directory and linked to the same Tinsukia office as the current company records. Snov verification did not finish during the 20:30 IST check. The address remains out of the tracker and campaign until Snov returns a final result.
