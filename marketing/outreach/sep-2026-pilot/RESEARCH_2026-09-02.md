- The government Assam Tea Statistics 2023 report provides district totals only, not estate-level acreage. It was not used to qualify individual accounts.
- Current-client, prior-rejection, active-sales and suppression registers were not available from a verified live source during this run.
- Every added account remains blocked. No contact or export row was produced.
