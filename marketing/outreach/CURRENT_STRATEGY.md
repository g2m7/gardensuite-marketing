## 1. What the market is like

Evidence: [docs/market/TEA_ERP_MARKET_2026-09.md](../../docs/market/TEA_ERP_MARKET_2026-09.md) and [north-bengal-intelligence/reopening/README.md](north-bengal-intelligence/reopening/README.md).

- **Trust is very low.** Owners buy from people they already know, or from someone a trusted person names. They don't buy from a stranger's email. A referral from a customer has already produced a sale. Cold email produced no recorded replies in September.
- **Buyers.** The owner, director or head office (Siliguri, Kolkata, Guwahati, Dibrugarh) approves and pays. The garden manager has strong influence and can block adoption. Some managers resist independent checks, so we sell to owners and treat the manager as the daily user.
- **Competitors sell the same features.** Softweb (PACE ERP and Blueeye), TEAlink, TeaMS and Iplusdigital all claim face attendance, Bluetooth scales and offline capture. Softweb claims 175+ plantation companies and markets actively. GardenSuite can't win on the feature list. We win on trust, local presence and records an owner can show to others.
- **Influencers.** Customers, garden managers who move between estates, PF consultants, CA firms, visiting agents, brokers, associations (ITA branches, TAI, DBITA, TIPA, NETA, ABLTMA, ATPA) and trade events.
- **Urgency right now:**
  - The labour codes took effect on 21 November 2025.
  - West Bengal has threatened FIRs over unpaid PF and gratuity (July 2026).
  - Assam wages rose Rs 30 from 1 April 2026.
  - Puja bonus season runs from August to October.
- **Reopening gardens in North Bengal.** On 25 September 2026 the state ordered closed gardens to reopen within 7 to 10 days. It counts 21 fully closed and 11 partly closed gardens. A task force will look for new operators, and new operators must clear PF and worker dues. A new or returning operator needs attendance, wage and PF records from the first week.

## 2. Why the September plan failed

- It spent three weeks on gates and paperwork. The repo records only 9 estates emailed. The owner reports 20.
- Cold email to strangers is the weakest channel in a low-trust market, and the plan used it alone.
- It targeted only Dibrugarh and Tinsukia, 500+ km from Bagdogra, where local support counts least. It left 291 qualified West Bengal accounts unused.
- It led with face attendance, which every competitor also sells.
- The campaign had no proof behind it: no named reference, no site visit and no case study.

## 3. Goal

1. **Revenue.** Close sales through the channels that build trust fastest.
2. **Top 3 visibility.** When any Indian tea estate looks for a tea ERP or tea garden software, GardenSuite is one of the first three names it finds, hears about and trusts.

"Top 3" is measured by the number of target search terms where GardenSuite ranks on page one, the number of directories and industry lists that name it, industry events attended, and shortlist rate. Shortlist rate is how often a prospect says GardenSuite is among the vendors it is comparing. Details are in [docs/market/TOP3_VISIBILITY_PLAN.md](../../docs/market/TOP3_VISIBILITY_PLAN.md).

## 4. Positioning

Core line: **GardenSuite by Sarbani Associates, Bagdogra. Tea garden software since 2000, used by 20+ estates. It gives owners hazira, leaf-weight, wage and PF records they can check and show.**

- Lead with records the owner can trust and show to others: head office, auditors, PF inspectors, lenders, the Labour Department and workers. Face attendance, smart weighing and offline capture are the mechanism.
- Lead with local people: a team that visits, trains staff and answers the phone. This is strongest in North Bengal. In Assam, back it with named visit dates.
- Small first step: the Puja package trial (GS Face plus office review) or a Tea Loss Audit. Full ERP comes later.
- Never attack a competitor. Say that an estate on older software is welcome to compare.

Claim rules from `product.md` still apply: say "helps stop proxy attendance", invent no savings figures, and name no client without written permission.

## 6. Channels in priority order

1. **Paid referrals.** Cash commission is approved in principle. The structure is not chosen yet. The options are in [referrals/README.md](referrals/README.md). Referrers include customers, managers who move between estates, PF consultants, CA firms, scale and electronics dealers and suppliers.
2. **See it working.** Take a serious prospect to a customer garden that has agreed to host the visit, or show a recorded walkthrough of a live garden. This is the strongest proof in a low-trust market. Host gardens need written permission and a thank-you. See [referrals/README.md](referrals/README.md#host-gardens).
3. **Field sales visits and phone calls.** Hire or assign one field sales person, based in Siliguri, with planned trips to Assam. See [field-sales/README.md](field-sales/README.md).
4. **Cold email at volume.** Reach 100+ new estate accounts across North Bengal and Assam. Email only starts the contact. The real target is a phone call or visit. Plan for a 1% to 3% reply rate, not 7% to 8%. List and copy: [email-expansion-2026-10/](email-expansion-2026-10/).
5. **Visibility work toward top 3.** Search pages and comparison pages, directory listings, a small Google Search ads test, trade press, association presence and events. See [TOP3_VISIBILITY_PLAN.md](../../docs/market/TOP3_VISIBILITY_PLAN.md).
6. **Founder presence on Facebook and LinkedIn.** Posts and comments in groups, following each group's rules. This supports the channels above. It does not replace them.

Postal letters to a garden's registered address are allowed as a door-opener before a visit.

## 7. Operating rules

These are the only standing gates. Everything else in this file and the linked playbooks is authorized for the owner and the field sales person to carry out.

- **Estate is the account.** Link contacts, touches, referrals and outcomes to the estate record.
- **Do Not Contact overrides every channel.** Suppress immediately on stop, unsubscribe, complaint or hard bounce. A new source file never reactivates a suppressed contact.
- **Existing customers are never cold-contacted.** The client list is in `AGENTS.md`. Referral asks to customers go through the existing relationship.
- **Email:**
  - Use only addresses found in a source. Never pattern-guess an address.
  - Send only to addresses with an external `safe`/`deliverable` result or Snov `Valid`.
  - No attachments in the first email.
  - Include an easy stop line.
  - Keep to about 12 automated emails per mailbox per day, follow-ups included.
- **Phone and WhatsApp:**
  - Calls are one-to-one, in business hours, and only to published business numbers or numbers given by a referrer with the person's agreement.
  - WhatsApp is for one-to-one follow-up after a call, a reply or an introduction. No bulk WhatsApp blasts.
  - Check current TRAI commercial-communication rules before any calling at volume.
- **Money:**
  - Pay commission only under a written referral agreement, and only after the referred estate pays.
  - Never pay the buying estate's own staff without the buying owner's written knowledge.
  - Never offer anything to government officials, task force members or union office-bearers.
- **Proof:**
  - Name a client, run a site visit or use a quotation only with written permission.
  - Otherwise say: "Many estates keep software details private."
  - Keep Longview, Subhasini, Debpara and Chandan out of all proof and marketing while their PF or closure news is live.
- **Data:** Collect public or introduced business contacts only. Keep prospect personal data out of public pages, URLs, analytics and screenshots.
- **Agents** may research, qualify, build lists and draft copy. Agents do not send, publish, spend or activate a campaign unless the owner instructs that specific action.
- **Owner approval is needed only for:**
  - new spend above the budget recorded in the action plan
  - naming a client in public
  - each commission payout
  - price quotes outside the standard package
