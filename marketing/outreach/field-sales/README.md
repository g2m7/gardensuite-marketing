# Field sales: role, routine and scorecard

Status: CURRENT (under [CURRENT_STRATEGY.md](../CURRENT_STRATEGY.md))
Updated: 2026-09-27 | Owner: Kaushik Majumder

The owner has decided to hire or assign one field sales person. In a low-trust market a face at the garden gate and a phone call from a known number move deals faster than email. This file covers the role, the weekly routine, what to say at a visit and how to measure the work.

## Role brief

**Title:** Field Sales Executive, GardenSuite (Sarbani Associates)
**Base:** Siliguri or Bagdogra. Covers Dooars, Terai and Darjeeling weekly, and Upper Assam in planned trips.

Good fit:

- Has worked in or around tea gardens, for example as a garden clerk, assistant, payroll staff, supplier sales rep or scale dealer.
- Speaks Bengali and Hindi. Assamese or Nepali is a plus.
- Owns a two-wheeler or can drive. Comfortable travelling 3 to 4 days a week.
- Can run the demo on a phone or tablet after one week of training.
- Honest. In this market one broken promise is remembered for years.

Pay (proposal, owner to set):

- A fixed monthly salary and travel allowance.
- A fixed amount per completed demo or garden visit with the owner or manager present.
- A fixed amount per signed estate, similar to the referral fee in [referrals/README.md](../referrals/README.md).
- An Assam-based agent on commission only is an alternative for Assam, if one can be found through customers or dealers.

## Weekly routine

| Day | Work |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | Plan the week. Check the reopening news (see the [reopening playbook](../north-bengal-intelligence/reopening/PLAYBOOK.md)). Call back everyone due this week. Book 6 to 8 visits. |
| Tuesday to Thursday | Visits: 2 or 3 gardens a day in one area, such as Banarhat-Binnaguri, Birpara-Madarihat, Kalchini, Naxalbari-Bagdogra, Mirik-Kurseong or Chopra-Islampur. Call from the road to fill gaps. |
| Friday | Head office visits in Siliguri. Update the tracker. Send quotes. Ask for referrals from customers visited during the week. |

Assam trip: once every 3 to 4 weeks, 4 to 5 days in Dibrugarh, Tinsukia, Sivasagar and Jorhat. Book the visits two weeks ahead, using emails that got replies and phone calls. Announce the dates in the email follow-up with the line "I will be in Dibrugarh from [date] to [date]".

## Before each visit

- Check the estate in the tracker: owner or company, size, prior contact, old software, Do Not Contact status.
- Carry:
  - a demo phone with GS Face and sample data
  - a printed one-page sample report and wage sheet with sample data only
  - a business card
- If possible, call ahead: "I will be near your garden on Wednesday. Can I meet the manager for fifteen minutes?"

## At the visit (15 to 20 minutes)

1. Say who you are: "I am from Sarbani Associates, Bagdogra. We have made tea garden software since 2000."
2. Ask, then listen:
   - How is hazira recorded today? Paper, fingerprint, face, or other software?
   - How long does the office take to prepare wages each week?
   - Do leaf weight and hazira come together, or from separate registers?
   - Who decides on software? Is it the owner here, or head office in Siliguri or Kolkata?
   - Are you comparing any other software? Which ones?
3. Show one thing, not everything: face hazira offline on the demo phone, then the office review and the report.
4. Ask for one next step: a meeting with the owner, a host-garden visit, a trial or a quote.
5. Write down names, roles and the next action the same day.

Do not criticize the current vendor or the manager. Do not promise features, prices or dates you have not confirmed with Kaushik.

## Scorecard (review every Friday)

| Measure | Weekly target (after 2 weeks of training) |
| --- | --- |
| Garden or head office visits with a decision-maker or manager | 8 |
| Phone conversations (not just dialled) | 20 |
| Demos shown | 4 |
| Next steps booked (owner meeting, host visit, trial, quote) | 2 |
| Referral asks to customers visited | 2 |
| Tracker updated the same day | 100% |

Also record for every conversation which other vendors the estate named, if any. This is the shortlist rate used to measure top 3 visibility.

## First 60 days

- Weeks 1 and 2: hire or assign the person. Train them on the demo, the tea vocabulary and this file. Ride along on 5 visits with Kaushik.
- Weeks 3 to 8: run the full routine, with one Assam trip in week 5 or 6.
- End of week 8: review visits, demos, quotes and sales by segment and area. Keep what worked.
