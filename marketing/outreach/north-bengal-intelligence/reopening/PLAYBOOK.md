# Reopening gardens playbook (segment NB-R)

Status: CURRENT (under [CURRENT_STRATEGY.md](../../CURRENT_STRATEGY.md))
Updated: 2026-09-27 | Owner: Kaushik Majumder, with the field sales person

Research and sources: [README.md](README.md) and [reopening_gardens.csv](reopening_gardens.csv). Registry matches: `registry_match.csv`, when present.

## Why these gardens

- On 25 September 2026 the state gave closed gardens 7 to 10 days to reopen. A task force of the Tea Board and the Labour, Land and Commerce departments will look for new operators.
- New or returning operators must clear PF and worker dues. Many of these gardens closed because PF was deducted from wages and not deposited.
- A reopened garden starts wages in the first week. It needs a worker list, hazira, wage sheets and PF records at once. Buyers here decide in weeks, not months.
- Workers and unions distrust the operator after a closure. Records that show each worker's attendance and pay help the operator rebuild trust.

## The offer: Reopening Starter

A fixed first step, based on the Puja trial design in [docs/plans/puja-2026/01-offer-and-free-trial.md](../../../../docs/plans/puja-2026/01-offer-and-free-trial.md):

1. Worker master set up from the garden's list.
2. Face enrolment and field hazira on GS Face, working offline.
3. Office review and a weekly wage sheet.
4. PF and ESI registers from the same records, where configured.
5. A daily or weekly owner report.
6. Setup, staff training and support by the Sarbani Associates team from Bagdogra.

The owner sets the price and trial terms. Until then, say "we will quote after one site visit". No production face data is collected before the Puja privacy gate passes.

## Who to reach

- The promoter or director of the new operating company, often a Siliguri or Kolkata business family.
- In insolvency cases (for example Kalchini and Raimatang), the resolution applicant or bidder, not the resolution professional.
- The manager hired for the reopening, once the name is known.
- Their PF consultant or CA, who can introduce us.

Do not approach union leaders, officials or task force members to sell, and never offer them anything. Public meetings are for learning which operator is taking which garden.

## Weekly routine (every Monday, 30 minutes)

1. Check the sources listed in [README.md](README.md#how-to-spot-new-reopenings): Millennium Post, Telegraph, Siliguri Times, Uttarbanga Sambad, NCLT Form G notices and Labour Department notices.
2. Update `reopening_gardens.csv`: status, date, operator and source. Add a row for any new garden.
3. For each new operator:
   - Find the company in the North Bengal registry or through an MCA lookup.
   - Record the office city.
   - Ask our referral partners whether anyone knows the promoter.
4. Move the top five into the tracker as `Research` or `Contacted`.

## Approach sequence

| Step | When | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Warm path | Day 0 | Ask customers, PF consultants, CA firms and dealers whether they know the promoter. An introduction beats a cold call every time. |
| 2. Call | Day 0 to 2 | Call the company office. Use the call script below. |
| 3. Short letter or email | Same day | Send a one-page letter or email to the company office. Use the text below. |
| 4. Office meeting | Within 1 week | Meet at their Siliguri or Kolkata office. Bring the demo phone and a sample wage sheet and report. |
| 5. Garden visit | Within 2 weeks | Walk the muster point and the office with the manager. Note worker count, sections, internet and weighing. |
| 6. Written quote | Within 48 hours of the visit | Reopening Starter scope, price, start date and names of the support staff. |
| 7. Host-garden visit | If they hesitate | Take them to a customer garden that has agreed to host (see [referrals](../../referrals/README.md#host-gardens)). |

## Call script

> Namaskar, I am [name] from Sarbani Associates in Bagdogra. We make GardenSuite, tea garden software, and have worked with tea estates since 2000. I saw that [garden] is restarting. When a garden reopens, the first weeks need a clean worker list, hazira and wage records. We set that up quickly, with face attendance that works without internet. Could I meet you or your manager for twenty minutes this week?

If they say it is too early:

> Understood. When do you expect wages to start? I will call you a week before that.

## What to say and not say

- Say: records the owner can check and show; worker pay visible per worker; local team; setup within days, as scoped in the quote.
- Do not:
  - mention the previous owner's defaults or any police or EPFO case
  - imply government approval or task force backing
  - promise that face attendance will settle worker disputes
- Workers' welfare and FAWLOI data belong to the Labour Department. Never ask for or use them.
