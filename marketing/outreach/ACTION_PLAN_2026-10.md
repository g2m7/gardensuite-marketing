| Day | Work |

## Week 1 (29 Sep to 3 Oct): proof, replies, hiring

1. Check the `kaushik@getgardensuite.in` inbox and Snov campaign `3135559` directly. Record the real reply count from the September campaign in `sep-2026-pilot/daily-status.md`.
2. Ask 3 happy customers, in person or by phone, for written permission for: a public name, a private reference call, a host-garden visit and a short video. Each is a separate yes or no.
3. Choose the referral commission option in [referrals/README.md](referrals/README.md) and set the amounts.
4. Start hiring or assigning the field sales person. Role brief in [field-sales/README.md](field-sales/README.md).
5. Validate the emails in `email-expansion-2026-10/needs_validation.csv` with OrbiSearch or Snov.
6. Recheck the status of the top 10 reopening gardens in `north-bengal-intelligence/reopening/reopening_gardens.csv`. Find the new operator companies through the registry or MCA lookups.

## Weeks 2 to 3 (6 to 17 Oct): start contacting

1. Load the first cleared email batch (about 4 new estates a day, maximum 12 automated emails a day including follow-ups). Use the sequence in `email-expansion-2026-10/SEQUENCE.md`.
2. Call every reopening-garden operator identified. Send the one-page letter the same day.
3. Field sales person shadows Kaushik on 5 visits.
4. Sign the first 3 referral partners in writing.
5. Google Business Profile for Sarbani Associates, Bagdogra. IndiaMart and JustDial listings.
6. Email the digifysoft and sagesoftware list authors.

## Weeks 4 to 6 (20 Oct to 7 Nov): build presence

1. Continue email batches until all send-ready rows are loaded. Pause on any hard bounce until the cause is fixed.
2. First Assam visit trip: 4 to 5 days in Dibrugarh, Tinsukia, Sivasagar and Jorhat. Announce the dates to every Assam account that replied.
3. Publish the comparison guide and the estate-software page (see [docs/market/TOP3_VISIBILITY_PLAN.md](../../docs/market/TOP3_VISIBILITY_PLAN.md)).
4. Ask DBITA and TIPA for a short talk slot on labour code and PF record-keeping.
5. Ask Contemporary Tea Time for ad rates and offer the labour code article.
6. Record the new demo video (face attendance offline, then office review).

## Weeks 7 to 8 (10 to 21 Nov): review

1. Publish the labour code guide and the PF records guide.
2. First case study from a customer who gave permission.
3. Review by channel: referrals, reopening gardens, field visits, email, visibility. Keep what produced conversations.
4. Decide the Google Search ads test (Rs 10,000 to 15,000 a month for 2 months) with the owner.
5. Plan December: second Assam trip, Puja follow-ups, year-end wage work (a good moment to sell payroll).

## Stop rules

- Pause email on any hard bounce, spam complaint or provider warning until the cause is fixed.
