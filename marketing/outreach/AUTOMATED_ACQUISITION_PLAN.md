# GardenSuite automated acquisition plan

Recorded: 9 November 2026
Owner: Kaushik Majumder, Sarbani Associates, Bagdogra
Status: Discussion record and proposed build. No campaigns, spending or publishing activated.

## Owner direction

> I want to automate as much as possible. 100% automation.

The aim is to remove daily prospecting, posting, brochure delivery, qualification, follow-up and appointment administration from the owner's workload.

This direction changes the emphasis of the earlier [owner access review](OWNER_ACCESS_REVIEW_2026-11-06.md), which relied on personal introductions and arranged demonstrations. Introductions can still help, but the proposed system should operate without the owner having to arrange them every day.

The automation preference is confirmed. The channels, software, budget and live launch described below remain proposals. [CURRENT_STRATEGY.md](CURRENT_STRATEGY.md) remains the outreach authority, subject to the owner's latest explicit direction.

## Why the approach needs to change

The owner reports that cold outreach is failing. Visits often end with staff asking for another visit, without access to the owner or a personal contact. Finding the working head office is also difficult.

Brochure distribution, social campaigns and LinkedIn had not yet been tried when this discussion began. Existing playbooks do not establish that those channels have run or failed.

The proposed test lets an interested buyer request information and enter a demonstration flow. It avoids needing their private contact details before they show interest. Whether this brings suitable estate buyers at an acceptable cost still needs testing.

## Proposed flow

```text
Scheduled demonstration posts or a product advertisement
    -> Prospect requests details through WhatsApp or the website
    -> System immediately delivers the brochure and recorded demonstration
    -> Short qualification conversation
    -> Answers from an approved GardenSuite knowledge base
    -> Follow-up where permission is recorded
    -> Meeting booking, confirmation and reminders
    -> Estate account updated and results reported
```

The first practical target is unattended marketing through to a qualified booking. A completely unattended sale and rollout is unproven for GardenSuite. Bespoke pricing, negotiations, unusual questions and implementation still need an accountable person.

## What to automate

| Stage | Proposed automatic action | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content preparation | Turn approved product material into demonstration posts, captions and brochure drafts | Use verified product facts and permitted assets |
| Reach | Schedule posts on owned accounts through supported tools; run approved ads | Stay within named accounts, channels and fixed budgets |
| First response | Deliver the requested brochure and video immediately | Record the request and delivery; do not send duplicates |
| Qualification | Ask estate name, region, role, current process and main problem | Keep the flow short; do not demand sensitive estate records |
| Product questions | Answer from the approved product knowledge base | Route unsupported questions to the team |
| Follow-up | Send an agreed sequence and booking reminders | Stop on reply, booking, opt-out or escalation; a reply changes the conversation state |
| Booking | Offer available slots and send confirmations | Calendar access and actual availability must be connected |
| Account tracking | Link the contact and activity to the estate account | Check duplicates, existing relationships and suppression |
| Reporting | Report qualified enquiries, bookings, attendance and costs | Separate known estate buyers from general enquiries |

## First acquisition test

Test Facebook and Instagram demonstration ads that open a WhatsApp conversation, alongside a website response route. This is the recommended first experiment, not a proven channel for tea estate owners.

Suggested opening offer:

> See how face hazira and leaf weight reach the garden office. Get the short demonstration and brochure.

Deliver the requested material before asking qualification questions. Give managers a simple way to forward the same demonstration to the buyer internally.

Keep North Bengal and Assam equally represented in the test effort. Do not assume that members of third-party Facebook groups can be selected directly as an advertising audience. Audience availability and campaign settings need checking in the actual account.

Cold email can remain a supporting route under the current sending rules. Increasing unanswered message volume should not become the main automation project.

## Brochure and demonstration

Prepare one two-page brief and one short recorded demonstration using actual application screens and sample records.

Show a complete supported workflow:

```text
Worker face -> hazira -> leaf weight -> offline saving
    -> sync when connectivity returns -> office review -> supported daily report
```

Explain what needs internet. Offline field capture does not mean that an owner's online dashboard updates without connectivity.

Include Sarbani Associates, Bagdogra, tea garden software work since 2000, the approved 20+ estate proof point, setup and training, and a direct business reply route. Use client names or quotations only with the required permission.

## Channel limits

LinkedIn can support scheduled publishing through supported tools. Automated connection requests, unsolicited messages, scraping and automated comments should not form the acquisition system. LinkedIn restricts third-party automation of activity on its website.

Use the official WhatsApp Business Platform for the proposed conversation automation. Record permission for subsequent messages, respect the service window and template requirements, and provide a clear route to a person.

The earlier discussion checked these official sources on 6 November 2026. Recheck them during implementation:

- [LinkedIn automated activity policy](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1340567/automated-activity-on-linkedin?lang=en)
- [WhatsApp ads that open a conversation](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/create-ads-that-click-to-whatsapp/)

## Minimum build and rollout order

1. Prepare the accurate brochure, recorded demonstration and product answer library.
2. Build one inbound conversation flow with qualification and escalation.
3. Connect one estate-account tracker, including existing clients and suppression.
4. Add follow-up, booking and reminders.
5. Test the complete path with sample leads, including duplicate events, opt-outs and failed deliveries.
6. Run one approved paid acquisition experiment within a fixed loss limit.

Choose software after confirming the budget and account access. No CRM, scheduler, WhatsApp provider or workflow platform was selected in this discussion. Existing repository integrations are not proof that a live system is connected or ready.

Do not reactivate the archived Brevo nurture, WhatsApp queue or old lead-capture funnel as a shortcut. Those workflows need a separate explicit decision before reuse.

## Operation without daily supervision

Record each estate's source, qualification answers, message permissions, conversation state, booking and outcome. Use event identifiers so retries cannot send the same message twice.

Define the send limits, follow-up expiry, allowed accounts and spending ceiling before activation. Pause affected workflows on delivery failures, account restrictions, missing permission or broken tracking. Provide one switch that stops all scheduled sending and paid activity under the system's control.

An assistant must not invent prices, integrations, savings, client references or support promises. Escalate complaints, negotiations and questions outside approved material.

## How to judge the experiment

Track identifiable estate enquiries, qualified buyer conversations, booked and attended meetings, accepted reviews or trials, and paid sales. Include advertising, messaging, software and staff costs.

Record founder time spent per qualified meeting. The system should reduce the owner's daily work as well as produce useful conversations.

Views, clicks, brochure downloads and cheap messages are supporting figures. They do not establish that owners are considering GardenSuite. Automation success and acquisition success must both be measured.

## Decisions still open

- Total monthly budget for ads and tools, plus the maximum experiment loss.
- Available Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and WhatsApp business accounts and access.
- The workflow platform, tracker and booking service.
- Approved product material, prices and answers the assistant may use.
- Who handles escalations and attends qualified meetings.
- Exact sending, publishing and spending permissions for the live system.

The next decision is the combined monthly budget for advertising and tools. That determines how much reach can be purchased and how much the system must depend on organic discovery.
