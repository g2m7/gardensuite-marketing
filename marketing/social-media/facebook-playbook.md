This playbook outlines how to use Kinnor's personal Facebook profile and Facebook groups to generate voluntary conversations with tea garden owners and senior managers.

---

## Part 2: Facebook Groups Strategy

Facebook groups allow you to reach operational estate staff and directors who discuss field problems. However, group quality varies widely.

### Group Search Terms:
Search Facebook for:
- `Assam tea planters`
- `Tea estate owners`
- `Dooars tea planters`
- `North East tea industry`
- `Darjeeling planters`

### Qualification Criteria:

#### Skip Immediately If:
- 90% of recent posts are people selling loose tea, retail tea bags, or tourism packages.
- Dominated by job recruitment spam or generic motivational quotes.
- No posts or comments in the last 14 days.

#### Join If:
- Members actively discuss labor shortages, weather disruptions, plucking incentives, muster issues, or factory dispatch.
- Members have recognizable estate titles (Manager, Assistant Manager, Partner, Director, Proprietor).

**Goal:** Identify and join 2 to 3 high-quality groups. Do not join 20 low-quality groups.

---

## Part 3: First Group Post

Wait 24 to 48 hours after joining a group before publishing a post. Comment thoughtfully on 1 or 2 existing operational discussions first.

### Asset to Attach:
- [`gs_landing/static/screenshots/kamjari.png`](../../gs_landing/static/screenshots/kamjari.png) (section breakdown) OR a clean crop of the one-page owner hazira report format.

### Post Copy (No AI Slop):
```text
When you are away from the estate, does your daily hazira report show which sections are complete, or just a final head count?

We put together a one-page review format you can use with your current office team to check pending records and corrections before wages get calculated.

Happy to share the format with anyone interested.
```

### Core Rules for Group Posting:
1. **Never blame managers:** Do not say "stop manager theft" or "catch cheating staff." That offends managers and makes owners defensive. Frame it as "visibility when you are away from the estate."
2. **Do not demand contact details:** Never comment "DM me your phone number." Let people request the format openly.
3. **No duplicate blasts:** Do not paste the identical text into three groups on the same day. Vary the wording naturally.
4. **Disclose your affiliation:** If asked, state clearly: "I work with GardenSuite at Sarbani Associates in Bagdogra."

## Part 4: Moving from Comment to Private Conversation

When someone comments on your post (e.g., "Share please" or "How does this work?"):

### Step 1: Reply Publicly First
Keep the public reply friendly and helpful:
```text
Sent you a quick message with the format link!
```

### Step 2: Send a Polite Direct Message
```text
Hello [Name],

Here is the sample one-page hazira review format:
[Direct link or attached image of the hazira report]

It gives a quick way to review section completeness and pending field records before wages get processed.

Which estate are you managing or associated with?
```

### Step 3: Qualification & Next Step
- If they share their estate name, check it against our exclusion list (`marketing/outreach/CURRENT_STRATEGY.md`).
- If they are a qualified independent estate owner or manager, offer a short look at the mobile app:
  ```text
  If you ever want to see how we sync field face attendance directly into this daily report without internet, let me know and I can show you a 5-minute preview.
  ```
