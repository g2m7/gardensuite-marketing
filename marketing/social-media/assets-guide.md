# Social Media Assets Guide

Every social post must be paired with an authentic image. Text-only posts fail on social platforms. Do not use generic stock photos. Use the real app screenshots, reports, and photos already in the repository.

## Asset Usage Rules

1. **Keep it authentic:** Screenshots of the actual mobile screen and dashboard look credible because planters immediately recognize tea terminology (kamjari, hazira, challan, leaf weight).
2. **Do not add heavy filters or promotional borders:** No flashy sales banners, red arrows, or "50% OFF" stickers. Let the software interface speak for itself.
3. **Aspect ratio tip for Facebook:** Single images display best in 4:3 or 1:1 format. For two images (e.g. Mobile App + Dashboard), upload both together as a 2-image set.
