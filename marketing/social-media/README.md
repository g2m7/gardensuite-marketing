# GardenSuite Social Media Marketing

This directory contains the operational playbooks, profile templates, post copy, and asset mappings for organic social outreach (Facebook, LinkedIn, and Instagram).

## Strategy Overview

The goal of organic social is to start genuine, voluntary conversations with tea garden owners, partners, and directors across Assam and North Bengal.

Social is not for broad mass-marketing or brand awareness. It is a targeted channel to find and connect with the 10 to 20 estate decision-makers who need offline face attendance and reliable daily reporting.

## Core Rules

1. **Human first, zero AI slop:** Write like a person based in Siliguri speaking to a tea planter. No corporate bullet lists, no buzzwords, no fake drama, and no hashtags on personal posts.
2. **Always pair posts with real assets:** Text-only posts get skipped. Always attach an authentic screenshot of the mobile app, dashboard, or report template.
3. **No manager bashing:** Do not accuse garden managers of fraud or leakage. Frame all messaging around convenience and visibility: "clear figures when you are away from the garden."
4. **No public pressure:** Never ask for phone numbers or email addresses in public comments. Keep resources open. Move to private messages only after voluntary interest.
5. **Coordinate with outreach exclusions:** Never contact estates on LinkedIn or Facebook that are current clients, in active sales discussions, or currently receiving pilot cold emails without checking the canonical tracker.
6. **No em dashes:** Use standard hyphens only across all copy.

## Channel Priorities

| Channel | Priority | Working Routine | Primary Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook Groups | High | 2 posts / week + comments | Share useful report formats in qualified planter groups |
| Facebook Profile | High | 1 pinned post + profile bio | Acts as a credible landing page when group members click your name |
| LinkedIn | High | 5 connection requests / day | Direct search of tea estate owners and directors |
| Instagram | Low | 1 demo reel / asset post | Visual proof of the mobile app and smart scale |
| Paid Ads / Reddit | Defer | None | Do not spend ad budget or time here during initial phase |
