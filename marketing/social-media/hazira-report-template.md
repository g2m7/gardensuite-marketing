This document contains the suggested one-page daily attendance review format. It can be shared as a PDF, printout, or clean screenshot with tea garden owners and managers as a practical self-audit tool for daily estate records.

## How to Use This Asset in Outreach

1. **Self-assessment without software:** An owner can print this out or ask their garden clerk to fill it out for just one day.
2. **Reveals blind spots naturally:** When the clerk struggles to fill out "Batches Received in Office" or "Manual Fallback Exceptions", the owner immediately sees the value of automated, offline-first attendance.
3. **Low-pressure lead magnet:** You are giving them a useful template for their existing process, not forcing an immediate software purchase.
4. **Follow-up bridge:** After they review it, you can ask: *"How many hours does it currently take your office team to collect these numbers every evening?"* That leads naturally into demonstrating GardenSuite's automatic daily sync.
