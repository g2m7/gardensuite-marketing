# Profile Setup Guide

This guide details how to configure Kinnor Majumder's personal profiles across Facebook, LinkedIn, and Instagram to establish immediate credibility with tea garden owners and managers.

---

## 1. Facebook Profile (`kinnor.majumder`)

### Critical Prerequisite: Unlock Your Profile
- **Current status:** The profile is locked with the shield badge ("You've locked your profile").
- **Why this hurts trust:** When you post or comment in a tea planters group, interested owners tap your profile name. A locked profile shows an empty screen with no workplace, no bio, and no website. To estate owners, that looks like a fake account or spammer.
- **How to fix:**
  1. Go to your Facebook profile page.
  2. Click the three dots (`...`) next to "Edit profile" (or tap the shield alert).
  3. Select **Unlock profile** and confirm.
  4. Keep your personal photo albums or private family posts set to "Friends only", but ensure your **Intro, Workplace, City, Website, and Pinned Post** are set to **Public**.

### Facebook Bio (101-Character Limit)
Use one of these options:

- **Option 1 (Recommended - 90 characters):**
  ```text
  Tea garden face attendance & daily MIS | GardenSuite by Sarbani Associates | gardensuite.in
  ```
- **Option 2 (91 characters):**
  ```text
  GardenSuite by Sarbani Associates | Face hazira & daily reports for tea gardens | Siliguri
  ```
- **Option 3 (95 characters):**
  ```text
  Building GardenSuite at Sarbani Associates. Face attendance & daily reporting for tea estates.
  ```

### Public Intro Details
Set each of these fields to **Public**:
- **Workplace:** `Sarbani Associates` (or tag the official GardenSuite page)
  - *Position:* `Co-Founder / Software & Automation`
  - *Description:* `Building GardenSuite - offline mobile face attendance and daily reporting for tea gardens.`
- **Current City:** `Siliguri, West Bengal`
  *(Essential trust signal: North Bengal and Assam tea planters recognize Siliguri and Bagdogra as the operational hub for the tea belt).*
- **Website:** `https://gardensuite.in`

### Cover Photo & Profile Picture
- **Profile Picture:** A clear, well-lit daylight photo showing your face clearly at small thumbnail size.
- **Cover Photo:** Either a clean landscape photo of a tea estate or a simple banner featuring a tea garden background with text:  
  *GardenSuite | Tea Garden Attendance & Daily Management | Sarbani Associates, Bagdogra*
