# LinkedIn Playbook

This playbook defines the organic LinkedIn outreach strategy for identifying, connecting with, and conversing with tea estate owners, directors, and senior planters.

---

## Strategy & Operating Rules

- **Direct discovery:** Search by estate company names, proprietor titles, and regional keywords.
- **Daily cap:** Maximum 5 connection requests per working day. Focus on verified owners, not mass outreach.
- **No instant pitch:** Do not send an automated sales pitch immediately upon connection acceptance. Treat connections as professional contacts.
- **Defer paid LinkedIn ads:** LinkedIn requires a minimum audience of 300 member accounts. In a targeted geographic pilot (e.g. Dibrugarh and Tinsukia or mid-tier regional estates), reaching 300 accounts forces you to add irrelevant staff, diluting ad spend.

---

## Step 1: Pre-Contact Exclusion Check

Before sending any LinkedIn connection request, verify that the person's estate is NOT:
1. An existing GardenSuite client (e.g., Harishpur, Bagrodia, Simulbarie, Rheabari, Longview, etc.).
2. An active recipient in the live cold email campaign (e.g., Baghbari, Sadasiva, Satispur).
3. A large corporate conglomerate (e.g., Goodricke, McLeod Russel, Amalgamated Plantations).
4. An estate marked `Do Not Contact` in `marketing/outreach/` records.

---

## Step 2: Search Filters & Keywords

### Search Queries on LinkedIn:
- `Tea Estate Owner Assam`
- `Director Tea Estate Dibrugarh`
- `Tea Planter Dooars`
- `Partner Tea Estate North Bengal`
- `Managing Director Tea Plantation`

### Qualifying Roles:
- Owner / Proprietor
- Director / Managing Director
- Partner (frequently used for family-owned proprietary estates)
- General Manager / Visiting Agent

Confirm that their profile indicates *current* involvement with a working estate before reaching out.

---

## Step 3: The Connection Note

Always include a short, personalized note with the connection request. Keep it friendly, simple, and unpressured:

```text
Hello [Name],

I work with GardenSuite at Sarbani Associates on tea garden attendance and daily reporting. Glad to connect with others working in tea.
```

*(Note length: ~160 characters. Fits comfortably within LinkedIn's 300-character limit).*

---

## Step 4: After Acceptance

When an owner accepts your connection request:

### Do NOT:
- Send a 5-paragraph sales pitch.
- Pitch ERP software or massive overhaul.
- Ask for their private WhatsApp or phone number right away.

### What TO Do:
Wait 1 to 2 days, then send a short, helpful message:

```text
Thanks for connecting, [Name].

We put together a simple one-page daily hazira review format for estate owners who review the garden from outside.

Would it be useful if I share the link or PDF with you?
```

If they reply **Yes**:
Send the link or attach the one-page PDF:
```text
Here is the format: [Link / Attachment]

It highlights section completeness, pending field records, and corrections before payroll.

If you ever want to see how we sync field face attendance directly to an owner's phone without internet, happy to share a 3-minute sample.
```
