# GardenSuite Send-Ready WhatsApp Messages

This document contains field-tested first-touch WhatsApp messages across the 4 winning angles, plus the hardware replacement angle.

Every message adheres to these operational standards:
- **Length**: 25 to 70 words (fits on screen without triggering "Read more").
- **Language**: Natural Indian business English.
- **Punctuation**: Regular hyphens only. Zero em dashes.
- **Tone**: Peer-to-peer operator tone. No company biographies. No feature dumping. No AI slop.
- **CTA**: Low friction, permission-based.
