# GardenSuite WhatsApp Reply Playbook

This document is an operational guide for handling prospect replies in real time on WhatsApp.

**The Golden Rule**: Do not restart the sales pitch after someone replies. Answer their exact question directly, provide clear context, and ask one natural conversational question to progress the dialogue.

---

## 1. Core Principles
- **Be Direct**: If they ask where you are located or how much it costs, answer immediately. Never hide behind evasive sales tactics.
- **Short Messages**: Keep replies under 60 words whenever possible.
- **No Em Dashes**: Use regular hyphens only.
- **Progress, Don't Close**: The goal is to establish fit and arrange a 10-minute video demo or garden visit, not close an enterprise sale in chat.

---

## 2. Response Playbook by Scenario

### 1. "What is this?"
**Goal**: Explain the core utility in one simple sentence without jargon.

```text
It is an offline Android app and wireless scale for tea estates.

Supervisors use regular phones to mark worker attendance by face right in the section, and our scale links plucking weights automatically. No paper chits, no ghost hazira.

Do you currently use paper registers or cards for daily attendance?
```

---

### 2. "Send details"
**Goal**: Provide a concise summary with one relevant photo/PDF, not a 20-page corporate brochure.

```text
Certainly {{first_name}} ji. Here is the short version:

1. Android app: Takes worker face attendance in the field (100% offline).
2. Wireless scale (Rs 7,000): Logs plucking weight straight to the worker's name.
3. Office dashboard: Daily muster and leaf totals sync automatically for review.

Would you prefer a 2-minute video clip or a 1-page PDF summary?
```

---

### 3. "How much?" / "Price?"
**Goal**: Transparent pricing right away, anchored to our low flat tiers and Rs 7k scale.

```text
Very straightforward, {{first_name}} ji:

- Software: Flat annual fee based on size. Nano tier (1-2 phones) is Rs 10,000/year; Small (3-5 phones) is Rs 18,000/year. Dashboard included.
- Wireless scale: Rs 7,000 one-time per scale (1-year warranty).
- Training: On-site at your garden by our team.

How many field weighing points or sections do you operate?
```

---

### 4. "Not interested"
**Goal**: Graceful, immediate exit with zero argument. Protects reputation and leaves the door open.

```text
Understood, {{first_name}} ji. Thank you for letting me know. I will make sure we do not message you again.

Wishing you and {{estate_name}} a successful season ahead.
```
*(Action: Immediately mark account as `Suppressed / Do Not Contact` in tracker).*

---

### 5. "We already use software"
**Goal**: Discover what they use without attacking it. Identify if field attendance/weighing is connected.

```text
Understood {{first_name}} ji. Many estates we work with already had office accounting or payroll software.

May I ask which system you are running, and does it also handle offline face attendance and digital scale weighment in the field?
```

---

### 6. "We use Excel"
**Goal**: Validate Excel for accounting, show how GardenSuite feeds clean data into it.

```text
Excel is great for estate roll-ups, {{first_name}} ji.

Where we help is getting the field numbers into Excel without someone having to manually type paper chits every evening. GardenSuite exports clean, formatted Excel sheets ready for your clerk.

Would you like to see a sample export sheet?
```

---

### 7. "We already have an ERP" (e.g. Teapac, custom, or ADL)
**Goal**: Understand scope. Field capture can either feed their ERP or replace high-maintenance hardware.

```text
Glad to hear that, {{first_name}} ji. A proper ERP is essential for garden operations.

Does your current ERP capture worker face attendance directly at the section and link wireless scales, or do clerks still key in daily field sheets?
```

---

### 8. "Talk to my manager / accountant / IT person"
**Goal**: Get the correct contact name and number with permission to mention the owner's referral.

```text
Sure {{first_name}} ji, happy to connect with them directly.

Could you share their name and phone number? I will mention you suggested we speak so they have full context.
```

---

### 9. "Call me"
**Goal**: Confirm immediate or preferred call window.

```text
Will do, {{first_name}} ji. I will call you from this number in 5 minutes. If now is not suitable, please let me know a convenient time today.
```

---

### 10. "Call later"
**Goal**: Secure a specific time window without being pushy.

```text
Understood, {{first_name}} ji. Would later this afternoon around 4:30 PM work, or do you prefer tomorrow morning after your field rounds?
```

---

### 11. "Send brochure"
**Goal**: Send the 1-page visual overview PDF, not an overwhelming document.

```text
Sending the 1-page overview PDF right here, {{first_name}} ji.

[Attach: GardenSuite_Field_Workflow_Overview.pdf]

It shows the supervisor mobile screen, scale connection, and evening office report. Once you have had a glance, let me know if you would like a quick live demonstration.
```

---

### 12. "Send demo"
**Goal**: Offer the choice between a 2-minute recorded video or a 10-minute live screen share.

```text
Here is a 2-minute video showing the field supervisor marking attendance and weighing plucking leaf:

[Attach/Link: 2-minute GardenSuite Field Demo Video]

If you would like to test it on your own phone or see the office dashboard live, we can do a quick 10-minute video call whenever you have time.
```

---

### 13. "Who gave you my number?"
**Goal**: Transparent, honest source explanation. Zero evasion.

```text
{{first_name}} ji, your details are listed in the public tea estate directory / MCA company filings associated with {{estate_name}}.

We only reach out directly to tea estate directors and owners regarding labor attendance and field weighing. If you prefer not to be contacted on WhatsApp, please tell me and I will remove your number immediately.
```

---

### 14. "Where are you located?"
**Goal**: Emphasize local regional presence and deep tea heritage.

```text
We are based in Bagdogra, Siliguri (Sarbani Associates). We have been developing software for tea gardens since 2000.

Our technical team visits gardens across Dibrugarh, Tinsukia, and North Bengal regularly for on-site installation and supervisor training.
```

---

### 15. "Do you have clients in our industry?"
**Goal**: State verified proof while respecting industry confidentiality.

```text
Yes, {{first_name}} ji. GardenSuite is running across 20+ commercial tea estates in Assam, Dooars, Terai, and Darjeeling.

Many estate owners keep their software setup confidential, but we are happy to share relevant regional references once we discuss your specific workflow.
```

---

### 16. "Does this integrate with our existing system?"
**Goal**: Explain clean integration boundaries and standard export formats.

```text
Yes, {{first_name}} ji. GardenSuite generates standard Excel and CSV outputs formatted specifically for your existing payroll or ERP system.

Your field supervisors get the benefit of offline face capture, and your office clerk imports clean records without re-typing. What software are you currently running?
```

---

### 17. "Too expensive"
**Goal**: Re-anchor against actual hardware costs and wage leakage savings.

```text
I understand {{first_name}} ji. We kept pricing deliberately low compared to legacy systems:

Our wireless scale is Rs 7,000 (competitors charge Rs 15,000 to Rs 25,000 for terminals), and the software starts at Rs 10,000/year for 1-2 devices. Stopping just 2 or 3 ghost haziras a month covers the entire software fee.

You can even start with a single scale to test it out.
```

---

### 18. "Maybe later" / "Contact after season"
**Goal**: Note timeline, confirm exact month, set calendar reminder.

```text
Understood, {{first_name}} ji. Peak season keeps everyone occupied.

Would it be better if I check back with you in November during the end-of-season review, or early next year before first flush pruning?
```

---

### 19. Message read (blue ticks) but no reply
**Goal**: Do not badger. Wait 3 to 4 days, then send Follow-up 1 from `followups.md` with fresh technical value. Never send "Did you see my message?"
