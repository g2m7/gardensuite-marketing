Core features • Page 1 of 2
The 10-second pitch
GardenSuite runs face attendance and plucking leaf weight on an Android phone, right in your field. It works fully offline with no SIM or internet, and syncs clean numbers to your office computer before payroll.
What to say on calls and visits
1
Face attendance on an Android phone
At morning muster, the kamdar points the phone camera at the worker. The app matches the face in under 2 seconds. Helps stop proxy attendance, buddy punching, and ghost workers.
2
Smart Bluetooth scale for leaf weighing
A digital hanging scale links wirelessly to the supervisor's phone. When the leaf bag is hooked, the net weight saves straight to that worker's record. No handwriting errors, no fake weight slips, no weighing disputes.
3
Works fully offline in the garden
No internet, no Wi-Fi, no SIM card needed in field sections. Face checks and weights save on the phone itself. Remote sections never stop working.
4
One tap sync to the office computer
Back at the office, one tap uploads all field records. Saves 2 to 3 hours of re-typing numbers from wet paper registers every evening.
5
Office review screen before payroll
The head clerk checks every attendance, leaf weight, and remark on the desktop screen. Your garden stays in control and can fix mistakes before wages run.
6
Direct link to payroll, ticca, and muster roll
Approved hazira and leaf weights feed straight into wage calculations, ticca incentive, PF, and ESI. Cuts wage preparation from days to hours.
7
Daily MIS summary for owners
Owners and managers open a clean summary on any phone or laptop: today's turnout, total leaf plucked, and plucker averages. Stay updated even when traveling.

Objection handling • Page 2 of 2
Common questions and answers
Q1 Our garden sections have zero mobile network or Wi-Fi. How will it work?
Say this: "It runs completely offline. The phone needs no internet and no SIM card in the field. Face checks and weights save on the phone and sync when the supervisor returns to the office."
Q2 Can pluckers cheat by holding up a photograph or punching for friends?
Say this: "No. The camera checks liveness using real depth and natural eye movement. Printed photos and phone screens are rejected. It helps stop proxy attendance."
Q3 Why face attendance instead of thumb fingerprint scanners?
Say this: "Pluckers have rough skin, wet fingers, and tea leaf stains. Thumb scanners fail constantly in tea gardens. Face attendance needs no touching at all."
Q4 Can our older babus and field kamdars learn the app?
Say this: "Yes. The app has large buttons and simple steps built for tea garden staff. Sarbani Associates comes to your garden and trains your team in person."
Q5 We already use old software or paper books. Why change?
Say this: "You don't have to change everything at once. Start with face attendance and smart weighing to stop leaf weight leaks, and we link the numbers into your existing payroll."
Q6 What if a phone battery dies or the scale hook breaks?
Say this: "Any spare Android phone works as backup immediately. The scales are rugged industrial units, and we provide local repair and replacement from Siliguri."
