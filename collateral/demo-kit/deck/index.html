Daily records you cannot fully trust

These problems start in the field and show up at payroll.

Hazira is marked for a worker who did not come.

Loose weight chits

Weight slips travel with the leaf and reach the office late.

Payroll doubts

Disputes follow when attendance and weight do not match.

THE CHAIN

From garden to dashboard in seven steps

Each step is one record, checked at the source.

Face check

The app confirms which worker reported.

Scale weight

The Bluetooth scale sends the weight.

Saved on phone

The record links face, section and net weight.

Sync

Records move to the office when internet returns.

Office review

Staff check records and correct mistakes before payroll.

Payroll

Wages register, kamjari, PF statement and payslips.

THE KIT

What sits at the weighing point

Four things at the weighing point. The phone app ties them together.

  • Android phone

    Runs the GS Face app and stores every record.

  • Bluetooth hanging scale

    Sends the leaf weight to the phone without writing.

  • The supervisor

    Starts the session, checks the match and confirms the save.

  • The worker

    Looks at the camera once and hangs the bag.

PROBLEMS IN THE FIELD

When something goes wrong

Each problem has a clear next step. The fallback is agreed during setup.

01

Face not matched

Tap Retry. If it still fails, use the fallback agreed at setup.

02

Scale not connected

Reconnect the scale. If it still fails, use the fallback agreed at setup.

03

No internet

Records stay on the phone and sync when the signal returns.

04

New or returning worker

Enrol a new worker, or re-enrol a worker whose face check keeps failing.
