# Demo Kit Brief (shared context for all writers)

## Why this kit exists

Sarbani Associates runs live demo calls (Google Meet, Zoom or WhatsApp video) with tea garden owners and managers. On a call it is hard to show the Android field app and the Bluetooth weighing scale: the phone screen is small, the camera shakes, and the scale is a physical device. This kit replaces "point the phone at the camera" with a clear, screen-shareable slide deck that walks through the real app screens step by step, plus a talk track and call checklist.

The deck is an HTML presentation (16:9). Every app step is shown in a phone frame with the real screenshot and a short caption. The presenter presses the arrow key to move one step at a time.

## Audience

Tea garden owners, directors, general managers, estate and garden managers in Assam, Dooars, Terai, Darjeeling, Coochbehar, Uttar Dinajpur and Jalpaiguri. Many are not fluent in English. They are busy and sceptical of software vendors.

## Product facts you may use (nothing else)

- GardenSuite is a tea garden management system by Sarbani Associates, Bagdogra, Siliguri.
- Tea garden software work began in 2000. Used by 20+ tea estates across 7 tea-growing regions. Face attendance app launched in 2022. Version 3 (2026) added the advanced face app and the online dashboard.
- Field app (GS Face, Android): supervisor starts a session by choosing section, task (kg) and activity (Leaf Plucking, Pruning, Spraying). The app checks the worker's face (liveness and quality checks), then records hazira. For plucking, a Bluetooth scale sends the weight; the app links gross weight, bag deduction and net weight to the face-verified worker in one step.
- The app shows session time, task, count of records, section and bag deduction percent. Supervisor can switch activity or section and log weather during a session.
- Punch attendance: clock in and clock out with section and punch code (work code).
- Worker enrolment: choose unit, choose worker, capture three face photos (front, left, right), review and confirm. Done once per worker.
- Records are saved on the phone when there is no internet and sync when internet returns. The Sync screen shows last sync, synced/rejected counts and pending records.
- Phone reports: session list, worker search with totals per session and per day, session detail with export.
- Office (desktop ERP): office staff review field records, correct where needed, then use them for payroll: wages register, kamjari, plucking summary, PF statement, payslips. Also stores and factory despatch.
- Online dashboard (MIS): owners and managers see green leaf plucked, mandays, kg per plucker, wages summary, salary payment, from phone, tablet or laptop. Data comes from the garden systems after sync. Do not call it real-time or live.
- Rollout: Sarbani Associates visits, assesses current records, sets up devices, enrols workers, trains staff and supports. The first step can be small: one division, face attendance plus office review, or a Tea Loss Audit using the estate's own records. Full ERP can come later.
- Exact fallback steps (camera problem, scale problem, worker not matched) are confirmed during setup. The app has Retry and configured fallback options.

- Core positioning: records the owner can trust and show to head office, auditors, PF inspectors, lenders, the Labour Department and workers. Face check, smart weighing and offline capture are how it works.

## Hard writing rules

- Simple English. Short sentences. Words a busy garden owner understands in one reading.
- Never use an em dash or en dash. Use a regular hyphen "-" or a new sentence.
- Tea garden words first: hazira, plucking, leaf weight, section, muster roll, kamjari, PF, ESI, made tea, proxy attendance.
- Say "helps stop proxy attendance", never "stops" or "eliminates".
- Banned words: ecosystem, transformation, next-gen, frictionless, empower, seamless, optimize, cutting-edge, AI-powered, enterprise-grade, scalable, revolutionize, game-changer, unlock, leverage, robust, streamline, effortless, real-time, live data, zero paper, 100%, no errors, guaranteed, instantly.
- No invented numbers: no savings, ROI, accuracy percent, speed in seconds, worker counts, prices.
- Never name a client estate.
- No throat-clearing ("In today's world..."), no "not just X, it's Y", no colon reveals, no rhetorical questions as headings, no exclamation marks.
- Headlines state what happens. Example good: "The scale sends the weight to the phone". Example bad: "Smart weighing, reimagined".
