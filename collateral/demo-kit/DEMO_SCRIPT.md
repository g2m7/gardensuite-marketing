## Before you share the screen

Spend the first 2 minutes on three questions. Write the answers down. Refer back to them during the demo, for example: "You said chits reach the office two days late. Watch what happens here."

1. How is hazira taken today? Paper muster, punch machine, or something else?
2. How does leaf weight reach the office? Chits, phone calls, a notebook?
3. Who checks the muster roll before payroll, and how long does it take?

## If they ask

- **Will it work without internet?** Yes. Records are saved on the phone and sync when internet returns. The sync screen shows exactly what is pending.
- **What if the face does not match?** The supervisor retries, and re-enrolment is quick if needed. Fallback steps are confirmed during setup so your supervisors know what to do.
- **What if the scale breaks or will not connect?** The supervisor reconnects it, or uses a configured fallback. The exact process is set during implementation.
- **Will my staff learn it?** The supervisor taps through a few screens per worker. Sarbani Associates does the setup and trains your staff.
- **We already use software. Why change?** First we look at what it covers, its age and support, and whether attendance, weighing, payroll and review are connected. If you are on older software, you are welcome to compare us against it.
- **Can you name clients near us?** Many estates keep software details private. We can arrange a reference with permission.
- **What does it cost?** It depends on the size of the garden and the modules you take. We confirm the price after the visit.
- **How long does setup take?** It depends on the size of the garden and the modules. We give you a timeline after the visit.

## Close the call

Ask for one next step, not both:

1. A field visit: "Can I come to the garden this week? You will see the weighing on your own workers."
2. Or a Tea Loss Audit: "Send us one month of your records. We will show you where the records do not match, using your own papers."

The visit is the stronger ask if they are nearby. The audit suits a careful owner who wants proof first.
