# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Tea garden managers, estate owners, and senior staff across Assam, Dooars, Terai, Darjeeling, Coochbehar, Uttar Dinajpur, and Jalpaiguri. Many are not fluent in English. They need software that feels reliable, approachable, and built specifically for their industry - not generic SaaS dressed in green.

## Product Purpose

GardenSuite is a complete tea garden management ERP built by Sarbani Associates (serving since 2000). It handles face recognition attendance, smart wireless weighing, payroll, stores, factory production, and daily MIS reporting. The key promise: works offline at the garden, syncs to cloud for owner visibility anywhere.

## Brand Personality

Reliable, human, rooted. The software feels like a trusted local partner, not a distant tech company. Confident without being arrogant. Simple without being simplistic. Proud of 25 years serving the same community.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS template with abstract 3D illustrations and "AI-powered" buzzwords
- Corporate enterprise software aesthetics (SAP, Oracle vibes)
- Overly playful/startup-y fintech landing pages with cartoon illustrations
- Editorial-magazine aesthetics (serif headlines, mono labels, ruled separators) - this is industry software, not a design magazine
- Dark mode dashboards with neon accents - gardens operate in daylight

## Design Principles

1. **Trust through specificity** - Show real tea garden context, not generic business imagery. Every visual element should reinforce "this was built for tea gardens."
2. **Clarity over cleverness** - The audience includes non-English-fluent users. Simple language, obvious hierarchy, no decorative ambiguity.
3. **Offline reliability as premium** - Position offline capability as a strength, not a fallback. The garden is the real world; internet is the luxury.
4. **Local credibility** - Sarbani Associates is the trusted name. GardenSuite is the product. Parent company visibility builds trust in a relationship-heavy industry.
5. **Warm authority** - Green is natural here, but avoid clinical/corporate green. Think tea leaves, soil, growth - organic warmth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- Reduced motion respected (already implemented)
- Simple language for non-native English speakers
- Large touch targets for mobile usage in field conditions
- High contrast for outdoor/bright-light screen viewing
