02 / 11 - Why GardenSuite
The problem

Tea garden data is often split across registers, sheets, and delayed reports.

GardenSuite brings daily field work, wage calculation, stock movement, factory production, and MIS into one practical system. It is made for estate offices where internet may be weak and daily work cannot stop.

Manual registers slow the office

Attendance, plucking, issue slips, factory data, and daily summaries are often copied again before final reports are ready.

Reports arrive too late

Managers need daily costs, crop, labour, and stock position quickly. Delayed data makes cost control difficult.

Departments work separately

Payroll, stores, factory, and MIS need the same facts, but disconnected systems create repeated work and errors.

Garden internet is not always reliable

Estate offices need software that works locally first, then supports cloud viewing where required.

OneSystem
Simple, connected, and made for tea gardens. Start with the modules you need. Use it in a single PC or LAN. Add face attendance, Smart Weigh, and cloud MIS when ready.

04 / 11 - Face + Smart Weigh
Modern attendance

Verified worker, verified weight, one record.

GardenSuite works with the in-house face attendance app and Smart Weigh wireless hanging scale. It helps stop proxy attendance and reduces copying errors during plucking weighment.

  • Face recognition attendance - worker identity is verified by face, not by paper or memory.
  • Smart wireless scale - plucking weight can be linked to the verified worker in one workflow.
  • Offline first, sync later - field work can continue even where network is weak.

06 / 11 - Stores
Stores module

Know what came in, what went out, and what is left.

GardenSuite Stores helps estates manage suppliers, purchases, challans, bills, requisitions, issue slips, item stock, and consumption reports.

  • Purchase register - supplier data, item master, HO and local challans, bill update, and payment marking.
  • Issue register - requisition entry with request by, purpose, item details, and issue reports.
  • Stock reports - item at a glance, stock book, average cost basis, and date wise stock view.
  • Consumption reports - all stores consumption and chemical stores consumption for costing.
LessLeakage
Control stock movement clearly. Estate staff can see purchase, issue, purpose, stock, and consumption in one place, instead of checking separate registers.

07 / 11 - Factory + Bought Leaf
Factory module

Track factory run, green leaf, production, packing, and despatch.

GardenSuite supports factory records from opening time to final despatch. It covers own leaf, bought leaf, made tea, grade wise stock, packing sizes, invoice, challan, gate pass, and daily MIS.

Factory run data

Factory open and close time, dryer start and stop, roll start, and fuel consumption for withering and drying.

Green leaf

Own leaf entry, bought leaf entry, green leaf stock, withering, drying, and made tea reports.

Production and bulk

Bulk tea made for CTC, orthodox, or green tea, and own leaf or bought leaf consumption against made tea.

Sorting and grade stock

Grade wise sorting of bulk and grade wise stock control for factory reporting.

Packing

Lot number, serial number, gross weight, net weight, tare weight, bag stock, packet stock, and pack sizes.

Despatch

GST invoice, despatch challan, gate pass, transporter, auction sale, private sale, employee tea, and sampling.

DailyFactory MIS
Keep factory figures ready for management. Fuel, packing material, mandays, crop synopsis, grade percentage, sorting, packing, despatch, and green leaf reports are available for day-to-day review.
