## Product overview

**One-line description:** GardenSuite is a tea garden management system by Sarbani Associates that connects face-verified attendance, smart weighing, payroll, factory production, stores, and daily reports.

GardenSuite is built for the way tea gardens work. Field tools can save attendance and leaf-weight records when internet is weak. The office can review the records and use them for payroll and reporting. Owners and senior staff can check daily information through the online dashboard.

**Product category:** Tea garden ERP and attendance system.

**Product type:** Installed business software, Android field application, connected weighing hardware, online dashboard, implementation, training, and support.

**Business model:** Consultative sale. Modules and implementation are quoted according to the garden's size and requirements. Pricing is not public. The main conversion action is a free demo.

## Product modules

### Face Attendance and Smart Weighing

- Checks worker identity by face before hazira is recorded.
- Saves field records locally when internet is unavailable.
- Syncs records when connectivity returns.
- Connects a wireless scale to the verified worker record.
- Records tea garden context such as section, work, session, leaf weight, deductions, and quality where configured.
- Gives the office a review and correction workflow before downstream use.

### Payroll

- Uses attendance, leaf weight, rates, deductions, and applicable payroll rules.
- Supports tea garden payroll work including PF, ESI, bonus, and muster-roll needs where configured.
- Reduces repeated entry between field records and office calculation.

### Factory Production

- Records green leaf receipt, production activity, made tea, grades, dispatch, and factory reporting.
- Helps staff connect factory records and production costs.

### Store Management

- Records purchase, receipt, issue, balance, and section-wise use of store items.
- Gives office staff a clearer stock record than separate registers.

### Daily Report and Online Dashboard

- Shows daily garden information on phone, tablet, or laptop.
- Helps owners and managers review key numbers away from the garden.
- Uses information sent from the garden systems. Do not describe every figure as live or real-time unless the specific workflow is verified.

## Problems GardenSuite addresses

- Proxy attendance risk when identity is not checked.
- Attendance and leaf weight recorded in separate places.
- Paper registers and weight chits reaching the office late.
- Repeated entry between field, payroll, factory, stores, and reports.
- Internet-dependent tools failing in field conditions.
- Owners waiting for daily numbers from the garden.
- Staff uncertainty about setup, training, support, and what happens when equipment fails.

Do not claim a guaranteed financial saving or error-free operation without verified evidence.

## Differentiation

### Built for tea gardens

GardenSuite uses tea garden records such as hazira, plucking, leaf weight, section, work code, PF, ESI, made tea, muster roll, and daily report. It is not a generic office attendance tool with renamed fields.

### Offline field work

The field workflow saves records on the device and syncs when internet is available. Offline capability is a normal operating requirement, not an emergency feature.

### Face verification connected to work

Face attendance helps stop proxy attendance. The worker identity can be connected to attendance, work context, and leaf weight instead of ending at a clock-in record.

### Field-to-office workflow

Field capture, office review, correction, finalization, payroll inputs, and reports form one operating chain.

### Local rollout and support

Sarbani Associates handles assessment, installation, staff training, and support. GardenSuite is sold as a working tea garden system, not as self-service software with no implementation help.

## Buyer concerns and responses

| Concern | Response |

| Will face attendance work for every worker immediately? | Worker enrolment, retry, re-enrolment, and permitted fallback processes are part of rollout. Avoid absolute accuracy claims. |
| What happens when the camera or scale has a problem? | The workflow includes retry and configured fallback options. The exact process is confirmed during implementation. |

| We already use software. Why change? | First identify what the current system covers, its age, support, and whether attendance, weighing, payroll, and review are connected. Do not attack the current vendor without evidence. |
| Can you name nearby clients? | Many estates keep software details private. Use a name only with permission; otherwise use region-level experience. |
