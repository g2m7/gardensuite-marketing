# Email Sequences

## Sequence: Attendance + Smart Weighing Guide

**Trigger**: A website visitor requests the guide and gives email permission
**Goal**: Start a useful reply or book a demo
**Length**: 7 emails over 12 days  
**Timing**: Every 2 days, Tuesday to Thursday at 10 AM local time when possible
**Exit Conditions**: Reply, demo booking, unsubscribe, hard bounce, or complaint

Do not add the consolidated cold-lead database to this automation. Imported contacts need a recorded existing business relationship, another acceptable B2B basis under Brevo rules, or direct permission before campaign email.

## Send Rules

- One main CTA per email.
- Include the Brevo unsubscribe link in every template.
- Stop the sequence after a reply, demo booking, unsubscribe, hard bounce, or complaint.
- Do not use fake urgency, fake limited slots, named client quotations, or results without written approval.
- Use `helps stop proxy attendance`, not an absolute promise.
- Do not auto-enrol imported leads without a recorded permission or acceptable business basis.

## Pilot Metrics

Measure delivery, unique opens, clicks, replies, demo requests, hard bounces, unsubscribes, and complaints. Review the first 20 to 30 approved recipients before increasing volume.
