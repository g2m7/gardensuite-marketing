Let us be honest. Manual attendance and weighing cause three big problems:

  1. Proxy punching - One worker marks for another. You pay for work not done.
  2. Paper registers - Damaged by rain, lost, or filled with errors. Payroll becomes a nightmare.
  3. Weighing mistakes - Manual entry errors mean wrong payments and worker complaints.

These problems cost gardens thousands every month. But the bigger cost is trust. When workers feel cheated, morale drops. Retention becomes hard.

GardenSuite fixes this with one simple system:

  • Worker stands in front of the tablet. Face recognised. Attendance marked.
  • Worker puts leaves on the scale. Weight recorded. Linked to their face.
  • Data syncs to cloud. You see live numbers on your phone.
