Research date: 2026-09-27. Method: public web pages and web search only. No logins, no paid tools.

## 1. Summary

1. The serious tea ERP competitors are Kolkata based Softweb (PACE ERP plus the Blueeye field app), TEAlink and Teapac (RDG Systems / Ray Computech). TeaMS ERP (Ooty) leads in South India. iSmart (Harvest WebOn) sells to large groups such as Apeejay, Finlays and Olam. Sources in Section 2.
2. Softweb is the strongest direct rival. It sells the same story as GardenSuite (face recognition with liveness, Bluetooth scale, offline field app into ERP), claims 175+ plantation companies, and names Goodricke and Amalgamated Plantations (Tata). Source: https://softweb.co.in/blueeye/ and https://softweb.co.in/pace/
3. TEAlink has the widest named client list in North Bengal and Bangladesh (Jalan group, Mohurgong & Gulma, Gangabari, Ispahani, Akij) and says brokers Contemporary and Parcon use it. Source: https://tealink.in
4. TeaMS ERP is the only vendor with public pricing: Rs 100 / 125 / 150 per employee per month. Source: https://teamserp.in/pricing
5. GardenSuite ranks well for narrow queries ("tea garden ERP", "tea garden payroll software", "tea garden face attendance app offline") but is missing for "tea estate software India", "plantation ERP India", "tea estate management software" and "tea factory software". See Section 3.
6. "Green leaf weighing software" is uncontested. Results are consumer scales, not tea vendors. A dedicated page could win this term.
7. Buying is referral driven. Associations (ITA, TAI, DBITA, TIPA, NETA, ABLTMA), brokers and head offices in Kolkata shape decisions. No vendor shows visible event sponsorship. Section 4.
8. The four labour codes took effect on 21 Nov 2025 and repeal the Plantations Labour Act 1951. Welfare duties stay, and in-kind pay is capped at 15% of wages. This is the biggest payroll trigger in years. Section 5.
9. Wages and PF enforcement are rising at the same time. Assam wages rose Rs 30 from 1 Apr 2026. West Bengal set a one-month PF and gratuity deadline in Jul 2026 with FIR threats. Section 5.
10. Best near-term moves for "top 3": comparison pages, missing-query pages, named proof, association presence, and a compliance-led offer. Section 6.

### 2.3 Competitor notes

- **Softweb is the benchmark.** Its Blueeye page has a comparison table against Easyweigh, IRIS and NFC methods. It claims "175+ plantation companies" and "200+ estates in 12+ countries", and cites an Assam group with 10,500 workers. Source: https://softweb.co.in/blueeye/
- **Softweb runs the most active marketing.** It has a blog, YouTube videos, WhatsApp contact, the satellite site decoo.in, and a scale vendor (indmanscale) that lists "Softweb Blue Eye". Sources: https://softweb.co.in , https://decoo.in , indmanscale.com product listing.
- **GardenSuite's feature story is not unique.** Softweb, TEAlink, TeaMS and Iplusdigital all claim face attendance plus Bluetooth scales. GardenSuite must win on proof, local support (Bagdogra), offline reliability and the leakage story, not on the feature list.
- **TEAlink has broker links.** If brokers Contemporary and Parcon really use TEAlink, TEAlink has a channel into estate head offices. Source: https://tealink.in (vendor claim, not confirmed by brokers) [UNVERIFIED].
- **Teapac looks dated.** Its blog was last active in 2020, but it has a long client list of Assam and Darjeeling gardens. Its clients may be ready to switch. Source: https://rdgteapac.wordpress.com
- **TeaMS sets a price anchor.** Rs 100-150 per employee per month. For a 1,000 worker garden that is Rs 1.0-1.5 lakh per month. Estates may use this to judge any quote. Source: https://teamserp.in/pricing
- **Listicles leave GardenSuite out.** The digifysoft "Top 15" tea ERP list names only generic ERPs. sagesoftware has a "Best ERP for tea industry 2026" page. Sources: digifysoft.com - top 15 ERP for tea industry; sagesoftware.co.in - best ERP for tea industry 2026.

## 6. Implications for reaching "top 3"

"Top 3" here means being among the first three vendors an estate owner finds, hears about and trusts.

### 6.1 Search

- Build pages for the missing queries: "tea estate software India", "plantation ERP India", "tea estate management software", "tea factory software", "green leaf weighing software", "bought leaf factory software". Section 3.
- Build compliance pages: "labour code compliance for tea gardens", "PF and gratuity records for tea gardens", "in-kind wage 15% cap tea garden". These match live triggers in Section 5.
- Publish a new, well-titled demo video for the face attendance app. The only current video result is from 2020.
- Check real volumes in Google Search Console before writing. This report did not use volume data.

### 6.2 Comparison pages

- Build fair comparison pages: GardenSuite vs Softweb Blueeye / PACE, vs TEAlink, vs Teapac, vs TeaMS. Softweb already runs its own comparison table (https://softweb.co.in/blueeye/).
- Build a "best tea garden software in India" guide that lists competitors honestly. The digifysoft list does not include any tea-specific vendor, so there is room.
- Use only public facts about rivals, with dates. Avoid claims about their weaknesses that cannot be sourced.

### 6.3 Directory and association listings

- Ask the digifysoft and sagesoftware list owners to add GardenSuite.
- Create an IndiaMart listing. Small vendors already sell tea garden software there.
- Check whether ITA, TAI, TIPA, NETA or DBITA offer associate or supplier membership, or newsletter listings [UNVERIFIED availability].
- Ask TAI about its website ad carousel.

### 6.4 Events

- ICC India Tea Conclave in Siliguri (May) is closest to base. Aim to attend or speak.
- ABLTMA AGM (April, Dibrugarh) fits the BLF plan.
- ITA AGM (August in 2026, Kolkata) and DBITA / TIPA branch meetings reach owners and managers.
- Time campaigns to Puja bonus season (August to October) and to wage change dates (1 April in Assam).

### 6.5 Proof

- Buying is referral driven, and rivals name large groups. GardenSuite needs named, permitted proof: 2-3 case studies with numbers (proxy attendance cut, leaf variance found, payroll hours saved) and short video testimonials from managers.
- Add a Contemporary Tea Time ad or article to reach owners through a broker publication.
- Keep the "many estates keep software details private" line for clients who decline to be named.
