# Top 3 visibility plan

Status: CURRENT (under [CURRENT_STRATEGY.md](../../marketing/outreach/CURRENT_STRATEGY.md))
Updated: 2026-09-27 | Owner: Kaushik Majumder

Goal: when any Indian tea estate needs a tea ERP or tea garden software, GardenSuite is one of the first three names it finds, hears about and trusts. Evidence for everything below is in [TEA_ERP_MARKET_2026-09.md](TEA_ERP_MARKET_2026-09.md). SEO detail and the six attendance search terms stay in [seo-pr-domination](../plans/seo-pr-domination/README.md). This plan adds the terms, lists and industry presence that plan does not cover.

## 2. Comparison and "best of" pages

Softweb already publishes its own comparison table. Buyers comparing vendors should find a fair GardenSuite page.

- `/tea-garden-software-comparison`: an honest guide to the main Indian tea software vendors (Softweb PACE and Blueeye, TEAlink, Teapac, TeaMS, GardenSuite). Rules:
  - Use only public facts about each vendor, with the date checked.
  - Say nothing negative that cannot be sourced.
  - Add a "how to choose" checklist: offline field use, local support, face and scale link, PF and ESI, factory, reports.
- Short "GardenSuite and [vendor]" pages only after the comparison guide is live and reviewed. Legal wording must be checked, and there must be no use of a competitor's logos.

## 4. Trade press and earned coverage

- **Contemporary Tea Time** (quarterly, Contemporary Brokers, Kolkata). Ask for rates. Offer an article on keeping hazira and wage records ready for the labour codes. Trusted brokers reach head offices.
- **North Bengal press** (Millennium Post, Telegraph North Bengal, Uttarbanga Sambad) and **Assam press** (Sentinel, Assam Tribune). Offer a plain, useful piece or a quote on how reopened gardens can keep clean PF and wage records. It must be information, not an advert. Never mention a client's troubles.
- Each time GardenSuite is quoted or listed, add it to a press page on the website.

## 6. Paid search (small test)

- A Google Search ads test on high-intent terms only: tea garden software, tea estate software, tea garden ERP, tea garden payroll software, tea garden attendance app. Target India, with North Bengal and Assam bid higher.
- Proposed budget: Rs 10,000 to 15,000 per month for 2 months. The owner must approve the amount. Setup and tracking follow [puja-2026/04-paid-media.md](../plans/puja-2026/04-paid-media.md).
- Stop or change it if it brings no demo request in 8 weeks.

## 7. Proof (needed by every item above)

1. Ask 3 happy customers for written permission covering, separately: a public name, a private reference call, a host-garden visit and a short video.
2. Write 2 case studies with real before-and-after facts the customer agrees to, such as office hours spent on wages or the number of proxy cases found. Use no invented numbers.
3. Keep Longview, Subhasini, Debpara and Chandan out of all proof while their PF or closure news is live.
4. Keep the line "Many estates keep software details private" for customers who say no.

## Order of work

1. Weeks 1 to 2: Google Business Profile, IndiaMart and JustDial listings, list-author emails, customer permission asks, Search Console volume check.
2. Weeks 2 to 4: comparison guide, the estate-software page, the labour code guide and the PF records guide.
3. Weeks 3 to 6: demo video, association talk requests, Contemporary Tea Time enquiry.
4. Weeks 5 to 8: the plantation ERP, bought-leaf factory and weighing pages, the paid search test once approved, and the first case study.
