# Custom Plan Framework

## How to Price Any Deal (4 Things Only)

We sell 4 things. Nothing else.

1. Weight Scale - Rs. 7,000 each (one-time, 1yr warranty)
2. Software Tier - Annual flat fee based on number of mobile devices
3. Training - Rs. 1,000/day + travel from Dibrugarh
4. Travel - Actual cost from Dibrugarh

---

Count the number of supervisors who need the app on their phone.

3. **Show software:** "The [Tier] tier is Rs. [Price]/year. Includes everything - app, dashboard, support."

## Competitive Talking Points

When prospect mentions **Applied Data Logix Easyweigh** or any RFID-based system:

### 1. "RFID cards don't stop proxy punching."
- Workers swap cards. Garden managers know this.
- Ask: "Have you seen workers sharing RFID cards?"
- Our face recognition verifies the actual person.

### 2. "Their terminals cost 2-3x more per point."
- ADL industrial terminal: Rs. 15,000-25,000 each.
- Our wireless scale: Rs. 7,000.
- For 10 points: ADL = Rs. 1,50,000+. Us = Rs. 70,000.

### 3. "Where are they based?"
- ADL: Chennai.
- Us: Bagdogra/Siliguri.
- For North Bengal/Assam gardens: same day vs waiting days.

### 4. "What happens if a worker loses their card?"
- ADL: Reissue cost, downtime, admin work.
- Us: Nothing. Worker just shows their face.

### 5. "How easy to add more points?"
- ADL: Buy another expensive terminal.
- Us: Buy another Rs. 7,000 scale. Pair to phone. Done.

### 6. "Is iris scanning really needed?"
- ADL offers iris as backup because fingerprints fail.
- Face recognition is simpler, faster, and just as accurate.
- Tea pickers don't need to stand in a specific position.

### 7. "Who owns the data?"
- Both: Client owns data.
- But we're local. Come to your office. Show you the export.
