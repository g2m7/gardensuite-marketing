# GardenSuite Pricing Structure

## What We Sell (4 Things Only)

1. **Weight Scale** - One-time hardware. 1 year warranty.
2. **Attendance App + Cloud Dashboard** - Annual flat fee per tier. Unlimited devices within tier.
3. **Training** - Per day, on-site at your garden.

Nothing else. No tablets. No stands. No routers. No UPS.

---

## Tiered Software Pricing (Simple Flat Fee)

One price per year. Unlimited mobile devices within your tier. Dashboard included.

| Tier | Max Mobile Devices | Price/Year | Ideal For | Margin |
|------|-------------------|------------|-----------|--------|
| **Nano** | 1-2 | **Rs. 10,000** | Small garden starting out | 70% |
| **Small** | 3-5 | **Rs. 18,000** | Medium garden | 57% |
| **Medium** | 6-10 | **Rs. 30,000** | Large garden, multiple sections | 54% |
| **Large** | 11-20 | **Rs. 50,000** | Big estate, group gardens | 48% |

### What's Included in Software Tier

- Face attendance app for all supervisors
- Wireless scale linking
- Cloud dashboard (attendance + weight reports)
- Offline mode + auto sync

**No per-device counting. No separate dashboard fee. One price covers everything.**

## Payment Terms

- **Hardware:** 100% advance. Scale ships after payment.
- **Software:** Annual upfront (best value) or quarterly.
- **Training:** Pay before visit.
- **Minimum:** 1 year software subscription.
- **Cancel anytime:** Cancel with 30 days notice before renewal. Keep the scale.

---

## What Is Included Free

- Remote onboarding (Nano and Small tiers)
- Phone and WhatsApp support
- App updates
- Cloud dashboard hosting
- Data export to Excel
- Scale warranty (1 year)

## What Is NOT Included

- Supervisor's mobile device (you use your own Android phone/tablet)
- Internet connection at garden (you arrange)
- Electricity at weighing point (you arrange)
- Travel for on-site visits (you pay actuals from Dibrugarh)

## Competitive Comparison: GardenSuite vs Applied Data Logix Easyweigh

Applied Data Logix (ADL) is our main competitor. Based in Chennai, established 1995. Their system uses RFID cards + iris scanner.

### Price Comparison (10 Scales Example)

| Component | ADL Easyweigh (Est.) | GardenSuite | Savings |
|-----------|---------------------|-------------|---------|
| Hardware (10 units) | Rs. 1,50,000 - 2,50,000 | Rs. 70,000 | 53-72% |
| Software (Year 1) | Rs. 60,000 - 1,20,000 | Rs. 30,000 | 50-75% |
| Installation | Rs. 20,000 - 40,000 | Rs. 8,000 - 25,000 | 38-60% |
| **Year 1 Total** | **Rs. 2,30,000 - 4,10,000** | **Rs. 1,08,000** | **53-74%** |

### Why GardenSuite Wins

| Feature | ADL Easyweigh | GardenSuite |
|---------|---------------|-------------|
| Attendance | RFID cards (swappable) | Face recognition (cannot cheat) |
| Backup biometric | Iris scanner (expensive, slow) | Not needed - face works perfectly |
| Hardware | Proprietary industrial terminal | Consumer scale + supervisor phone |
| Cost per point | Rs. 15,000 - 25,000 | Rs. 7,000 |
| Location | Chennai (far) | Bagdogra/Siliguri (local) |
| Add new point | Buy expensive terminal | Buy Rs. 7,000 scale |
| Lost worker ID | Reissue RFID card | Nothing - face is always there |
