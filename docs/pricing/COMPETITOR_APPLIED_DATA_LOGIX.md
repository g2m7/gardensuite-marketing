## Their Products

### 1. Easy Weigh / Easylog Weighing System
- Battery-operated data entry terminal
- Captures weight + worker ID
- Internal memory stores data
- Transfers to computer for payroll
- **No public price** - quote only

### 2. RFID Attendance
- Workers use RFID cards/tags
- Card tapped at weighing station
- Links weight to worker ID
- **Weakness**: Cards can be lost, stolen, shared (proxy punching still possible)

### 3. Iris Recognition Module
- Uses TCI311 module (Techshino, China)
- Added around 2015
- Specifically for tea plantations where fingerprints are worn
- Claims to "eliminate ghost workers and proxies"
- **Weakness**: Requires worker to stand at specific distance/angle. Slower than face recognition. Expensive hardware.

### 4. Android Advanced System
- Mobile app for data viewing
- Likely limited to specific Android devices they provide

### 5. Bought Leaf System (EASYLOG)
- For bought-leaf centres
- Wireless weighing

## Our Advantages Over ADL

### 1. No RFID Cards = No Proxy Punching
ADL's RFID system still allows buddy punching. Workers can swap cards.
Our face recognition verifies the actual person.

### 2. No Special Hardware Needed
ADL requires their proprietary weighing terminal.
We use a simple Bluetooth scale + the supervisor's existing phone.

### 3. Much Lower Cost
ADL's industrial terminals cost 2-3x more than our simple scale + phone combo.

### 4. Local Support
ADL is in Chennai. We are in Bagdogra/Siliguri.
For gardens in Dooars, Terai, Darjeeling, Assam - we can visit same day.

### 5. Easier to Expand
ADL: Need to buy another expensive terminal.
GardenSuite: Just buy another Rs. 7,000 scale. Use another phone.

### 6. Face > Iris
- Face: Natural, fast, works at normal distance
- Iris: Requires specific positioning, slower, workers may resist

## Sales Talking Points Against ADL

**When a prospect mentions they are considering Easyweigh/ADL:**

1. **"Do their RFID cards stop proxy attendance?"**
   - No. Cards can be swapped. Face cannot.

2. **"How much is their terminal per point?"**
   - Their industrial terminals cost Rs. 15,000-25,000 each.
    - Our scale is Rs. 7,000. Supervisor uses own phone.

3. **"Where are they based?"**
   - Chennai. If something breaks, wait for technician from Chennai.
   - We are in Bagdogra. Same day service for North Bengal/Assam.

4. **"What happens if a worker loses their RFID card?"**
   - Reissue cost + downtime.
   - With us: Nothing. Worker just shows their face.

5. **"How easy is it to add more weighing points?"**
   - With ADL: Buy another expensive terminal.
    - With us: Buy another Rs. 7,000 scale. Done.

6. **"Do you really need iris scanning?"**
   - Iris is overkill for tea gardens. Face recognition works perfectly.
   - Simpler = less problems.
