# Customer referrals

Priority: P1, continue | Owner: Kaushik | Estimate: 30 minutes weekly plus introductions

## Goal

Repeat a channel that has already produced a sale. Expect one suitable introduction from a willing customer; never assume two or more.

## Setup and workflow

1. Identify up to five customers with a working installation and no unresolved support problem. Use existing relationships, not a cold list of client employees.
2. At a successful review, ask for one peer who may find mobile attendance and daily reporting useful.
3. Ask the customer to check the peer's interest before sharing details. Provide a message they may adapt and forward.
4. Record the introduction against the recipient estate with source customer, permission, preferred channel and date. Check existing account history before follow-up.
5. Respond promptly after introduction, learn the buyer's need, and offer a short demonstration.
6. Thank the customer and share only the outcome the new prospect permits. Record permission separately if a public story becomes possible.

## Forwardable draft

I thought this might be useful for your garden. Sarbani Associates has a mobile attendance and daily-report package for tea estates. Would you like me to introduce you to Kaushik for a short demonstration?

The customer should use their own truthful experience if they want to add a recommendation. Do not supply a fabricated testimonial in their voice.

## Do

Make one specific, easy ask after value is established. Accept an introduction without needing a public endorsement. Track the referred estate separately from the referring estate. Keep any incentive transparent and agreed with the appropriate business.

## Do not

Pressure customers for multiple names, request an entire address book, privately pay an estate employee for influence, or use a referral to reopen a do-not-contact account. Do not let referral requests interfere with support.

## Goals and acceptance criteria

- Activity target: up to five eligible customer asks in 30 days.
- Outcome target: at least one mutually accepted introduction; this is a planning target, not a referral-rate forecast.
- [ ] Each ask records whether the customer agreed, declined or deferred.
- [ ] Each introduced person knows why Kaushik will contact them.
- [ ] Every introduction has a source, next action and estate match.
- [ ] Demo and paid-sale outcomes are attributed to the referral.

Review monthly. If a customer declines, do not keep asking in the same cycle. Continue measuring one referral at a time rather than trying to turn this into a broad affiliate scheme.
