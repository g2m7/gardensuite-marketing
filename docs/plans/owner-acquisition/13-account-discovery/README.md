# Estate, company and buyer discovery

Priority: P0 for cold email; ongoing for qualification | Owner: research preparer, with Kaushik reviewing final buyer fit

## Goal

Map a garden to a current buying entity and public business contact. Do not depend on a private mobile directory, a Tea Board disclosure or knowing the owner's residence.

## Setup and research precedence

1. Reconcile the estate against the existing client, rejection, suppression and active-sales evidence before selecting it for the cold pilot.
2. Establish the estate's identity and aliases from current business sources, credible public records, association/auction records or the estate's own material.
3. Find the operating company/partnership and connected group. Confirm that the named entity actually operates the estate, rather than assuming a similarly named tea brand is the owner.
4. Identify a current owner/director/partner or another relevant buying role. Use a current company source, professional profile with corroboration, public filing or a directly confirmed relationship.
5. Find a published associated business email, or use Snov discovery for the verified person and validate it. Record provenance and date. Do not pattern-guess an address and call it verified.
6. Check the preferred small independent-estate fit, actual group relationships, area and operating scale. Historical area records are context, not proof that all current facts are unchanged.
7. Place uncertain records in research with one specific next action. Do not substitute a famous large estate simply because its contact information is easy to find.

## Useful routes

Estate name -> auction mark/business record -> operating company -> current buyer -> business contact.

Estate name -> public company/professional source -> verified owner/director -> Snov discovery/validation.

Current customer -> willing introduction -> confirmed buyer relationship -> requested conversation.

A public garden address -> letter or agreed visit -> voluntary response. A registered office is not necessarily the operational HQ. Confirm a physical address before paying for a visit or shipment.

The [GTAC directories](https://www.assamteaxchange.com/index.aspx) provide garden/mark and broker research starting points. Directory information may be incomplete or stale; it is not a ready sending list. Public registry or directory access conditions must be respected. Do not bypass authentication or access restrictions.

## Existing preparation pipeline

Use marketing/outreach/sep-2026-pilot/prospects.csv and the pipeline's check output. The exporter already supports a cleared subset; its launchReady flag refers to a complete twenty-account list.

Do not require an exact workforce figure when the current rule allows a reliable scale proxy. Do not relabel a contact to pass a role check. For an owner-level Partner title that the script rejects, verify the relationship and make a deliberate code/rule change in a focused implementation task with appropriate validation.

## Do

Keep source URL/date, estate-company link, verified buyer role and rejection evidence. Record stale or conflicting information plainly. Recheck current sources before sending. Hand the owner a concrete final list rather than asking them to recreate the research.

## Do not

Collect private owner mobile numbers, infer missing decisions from archived strategy, restart rejected estates without a documented new fact, guess an HQ from a garden address, or treat a working email address as permission for every channel.

## Goals and acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each proposed cold recipient belongs to a confirmed estate and current role.
- [ ] Geography, area, scale and group checks have evidence appropriate to the pilot.
- [ ] Client, rejection, negotiation and suppression history is reconciled.
- [ ] Each email returns Snov Valid and is associated with the intended estate/person.
- [ ] Unknown records have a next research action; the final import includes cleared records only.
- [ ] Research continues toward twenty qualified accounts plus useful backups without making backups a prerequisite for a smaller authorized first batch.

Measure time per qualified account and contact coverage. If the main constraint is finding valid associated business emails, change the discovery method or use voluntary owner acquisition; do not merely add more uncontactable garden names.
