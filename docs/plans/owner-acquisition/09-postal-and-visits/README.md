# Postal letters and planned estate visits

Priority: P3 | Owner: Kaushik | Scope: a small named-account experiment

## Goal

Use a verified garden or public business address when an owner email is unavailable. Invite a private inbound response or an agreed visit without needing the owner's private HQ address or mobile number.

## Postal setup

1. Select up to five eligible estates with a verified deliverable garden/business address and no rejection, active-sales conflict or do-not-contact flag.
2. Address the letter truthfully to a named owner/director where verified, or to the estate office for the person responsible for attendance/reporting. Never label it legal, official or confidential to trick staff into forwarding it.
3. Use one page: a relevant owner question, the smaller offer, a sample report preview, the business identity and an easy reply route. Include a short readable website address as well as a QR code.
4. Give each campaign/source a non-identifying tag. Do not put the recipient's name or estate in a public tracking URL.
5. Record dispatch, delivery if available, replies, demos, printing/postage and time. A delivery receipt is not evidence the owner read it.

## Letter draft

For the person reviewing this estate's daily attendance:

GardenSuite from Sarbani Associates provides mobile face attendance and daily attendance reporting for tea gardens. Field records can be saved without internet and sent to the office when connectivity returns.

We have prepared a short owner hazira check and a simple daily-report format. You can view them at [short public URL]. If a demonstration would be useful, please contact Kaushik at [verified business contact].

Sarbani Associates, Bagdogra, Siliguri.

## Visits

Prefer visits by appointment or permission-based introductions. Combine suitable visits with existing service travel where practical. Confirm who will attend, the demonstration scope and whether the owner/director will participate remotely. Use a working offline demonstration and a clear next step.

If the office offers to forward information, provide it without pressing staff for a private owner number. A supportive manager can help assess implementation; the plan does not require them to sponsor an oversight purchase.

## Do

Verify addresses, use plain materials, respect office procedures, identify the business, and measure the whole cost of the visit. Keep a requested demonstration small and practical.

## Do not

Travel unannounced to pressure a buyer, pursue an estate after refusal, masquerade as government or legal mail, send expensive gifts, or infer that a registered office is the operational HQ where the buyer works.

## Goals and acceptance criteria

- [ ] Up to five recipients verified and cleared before dispatch.
- [ ] Letter, reply address and QR/link tested.
- [ ] All visits have a purpose and agreed access.
- [ ] Seek one qualified inbound conversation from the small test within thirty days; do not forecast a postal conversion rate.
- [ ] Cost per qualified conversation includes travel and founder time.

If five letters produce no relevant response, review address validity and message before another batch. Do not keep mailing indefinitely because delivery was confirmed.
