# Foundation: package, audience and proof

Priority: P0 | Owner: Kaushik with Sarbani Associates implementation lead | Estimate: one working day to document, plus technical verification

## Goal

Make the smaller package a complete purchase with its own operating value and support economics. Give owners a clear reason to enquire and a simple way to understand what they receive.

## Setup

1. Create a scope sheet containing mobile face attendance, permitted office review and the exact daily reports included. Mark smart weighing as included or optional. List minimum Android devices and any garden-office component.
2. Demonstrate operation without buying the full ERP. Document worker-master setup, enrolment, offline capture, sync, office corrections, access roles, export and the data needed by each MIS figure.
3. Separate attendance figures from payroll, stores or factory figures that require another source. Missing inputs must display as unavailable or pending, never as zero or an invented total.
4. Create one quote template. Record installation, devices, training, recurring service, travel, support, taxes, payment milestones and optional upgrades as separate items. Use actual amounts supplied by the owner.
5. Create a three-minute demonstration with sample records: capture hazira, show later sync/review, then show the owner's daily report. No production worker faces or estate figures in public material.
6. Prepare one permitted reference story or region-level proof. Ask permission separately for public name, private reference and site visit; one permission does not imply all three.
7. Keep a public contact route and a direct demo CTA. The micro magnet is optional for a buyer already ready to talk.

## Buyer message

For owners/directors: Check the day's attendance and understand which records have reached the office while you are away from the garden.

For supportive operational staff: Record hazira in the field and review exceptions without rebuilding the day from separate records.

Do not base public marketing on allegations about dishonest managers. Where approval must come from the owner, confirm that route during the sales conversation.

## Unit economics worksheet

Calculate contribution from the entry product before assuming ERP revenue:

- Cash collected for setup and contracted recurring service during the chosen measurement period.
- Less hardware, travel, onboarding labour, expected support labour and other direct delivery costs.
- Compare the remaining contribution against acquisition cost, including founder time.

Set maximum acquisition cost from the acceptable payback period and actual contribution. A larger later ERP sale is upside until its conversion rate is measured. Do not finance a loss-making entry installation with an assumed future upgrade.

## Do

Use hazira, attendance, section, work, daily report and sync when internet returns. Identify Sarbani Associates, Bagdogra/Siliguri. Give exact scope and costs before commitment. Show timestamps and missing-record status in examples.

## Do not

Promise live figures, universal integrations, fixed installation times or support SLAs without evidence. Do not describe the current office system as obsolete without confirmation. Do not invent annual savings or describe a workflow gap as proof of fraud.

## Goals and acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each included feature is demonstrated in the standalone configuration.
- [ ] Every MIS figure has an identified input, update frequency and missing-data behaviour.
- [ ] One quote can be completed without selling the full ERP.
- [ ] Direct delivery/support cost and a preliminary acquisition-cost ceiling are documented.
- [ ] Three owner customers can explain the smaller offer after seeing the sample.
- [ ] Public proof and sample data have the necessary permissions.

If the package still depends on buying the full ERP, resolve that boundary before public standalone claims. Other research and draft work can continue.
