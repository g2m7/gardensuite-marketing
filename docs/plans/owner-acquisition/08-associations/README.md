# Associations, small sessions and industry events

Priority: P4, optional | Owner: Kaushik | Effort limit: two hours initial preparation per organisation

## Goal

Test a concrete owner-facing session or invitation. Do not repeat the long-email circulation workflow that the owner reports did not work.

## Setup and bounded test

1. Choose one relevant organisation through a real connection or public office contact. Check the likely attendees and whether independent estates are represented.
2. Send a short session proposal: a 15-20 minute practical demonstration of what an owner can verify in the daily attendance report, with a sample report participants can keep.
3. Ask the organiser to provide a date, audience and invitation method. They can circulate an invitation without disclosing member contact data.
4. Offer at most one short invitation paragraph and a visual/sample. Decline or deprioritize a request for another lengthy marketing essay unless there is concrete evidence of an engaged audience.
5. Make one follow-up after seven working days. Without a host, date or credible next step after fourteen working days, park the route.
6. At a session, offer a private link/QR to the owner check or demo request. Attending the event alone does not opt everyone into marketing.

Public starting points include ABITA Zone 1 in Dibrugarh and the Terai branch in Bagdogra, listed in the [official ITA branch directory](https://www.indiatea.org/organisation). These are organisational contacts, not a promise of access or endorsement.

## Invitation draft

A short practical session for tea garden owners: checking worker-wise hazira, corrections and whether daily records are complete. Sarbani Associates will demonstrate the process with sample records and share a simple owner report format. Interested members can request details directly from GardenSuite.

## Events and site demonstrations

Consider a small owner session, a permitted customer-hosted demonstration or an industry event only after the audience and cost are known. Prioritize a speaking/demo slot over a large booth commitment. Obtain separate host/customer permission for visitors and photographs; use sample data unless operational access is explicitly approved.

## Do

Make the organiser's task short, useful and specific. Ask for voluntary participant contact. Record event source and actual conversations.

## Do not

Depend on the Tea Board releasing private information, buy membership lists, spend days preparing a long forwarded email, treat an association's logo as endorsement, or claim a confirmed event before the host agrees.

## Goals and acceptance criteria

- [ ] One concrete proposal has topic, duration, intended audience and host request.
- [ ] Effort and any cost stay within the documented limit.
- [ ] Any live event has a named host, date, permission and working private response path.
- [ ] Target one session with at least two voluntary relevant owner conversations; this is a test target, not an attendance promise.
- [ ] No attendees are auto-enrolled from a sign-in sheet.

No confirmed session after the bounded follow-up means park the channel. It must not delay the email or social experiments.
