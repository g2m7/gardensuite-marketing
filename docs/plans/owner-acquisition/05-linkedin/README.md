# LinkedIn organic discovery and messages

Priority: P2 | Owner: Kaushik | Estimate: one hour weekly plus relevant conversations

## Goal

Find current owner/director relationships and bring relevant people into the owner-check or demonstration flow. Use business identity and public professional context, not private mobile discovery.

## Setup

1. Make Kaushik's profile identify GardenSuite, Sarbani Associates and tea-garden attendance/reporting. Add the magnet and direct demo route to the appropriate profile links.
2. Search estate/company names and roles such as owner, director, managing director, partner, tea planter and general manager. A Partner title can represent an owner; confirm the actual estate relationship rather than guessing from the title.
3. Verify current employment/ownership with a second current business source where possible. Do not count an old profile as a current owner automatically.
4. Check existing estate history and record source URL, role, date and whether email is already active for that estate.
5. Prefer founder posts and relevant discussions before a sales message. Group discovery follows the same audience/rule checks as Facebook.

## Connection and message drafts

Connection: Hello [Name], I work with GardenSuite at Sarbani Associates on tea-garden attendance and daily reporting. I would be glad to connect.

After relevant interest: We prepared a short check for owners reviewing hazira away from the garden. It comes with a simple daily-report format. Would you like the link?

If they request it, send the requested link. Do not require a form if they already asked a direct product question; answer it. A connection acceptance alone is not permission for a long sales sequence or a phone call.

## Cadence and account coordination

Working maximum: five carefully selected connection requests per working day, only when there are relevant people and the account has no platform restriction. This is an internal cap, not a claimed LinkedIn safe limit.

Do not run an automated email sequence and unsolicited LinkedIn pitch against the same estate at the same time. Public content is fine; coordinate direct touches in the tracker. One unanswered relevant message does not justify repeated nudges across platforms.

## Paid LinkedIn

Defer. LinkedIn's current official guidance lists a minimum audience of 300 member accounts; meeting the minimum does not establish economic viability. Member Groups appears among its targeting options, but available targeting and regional restrictions must be checked in the actual account before setup. A tiny two-district audience may not support a useful test. [Audience guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a423690), [Targeting options](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a424655/)

## Do

Use a real profile, verify the current estate link, coordinate with email, and tailor the reason for contact to the person's role. Keep the first useful exchange short.

## Do not

Automate profile scraping or connection spam, use a fabricated mutual relationship, harvest mobile numbers, or treat group membership/connection acceptance as marketing permission. Do not buy ads just because a targeting field exists.

## Goals and acceptance criteria

- [ ] Profile and links accurately describe the smaller package.
- [ ] Ten relevant current owner/director profiles researched during the first two weeks of this channel.
- [ ] Every direct touch passes account-history checks and has a recorded outcome.
- [ ] Seek at least two useful voluntary conversations in thirty days; judge quality, not connection count.
- [ ] Role and estate association verified before a lead is counted as an owner.

If responses are consistently unrelated or absent, revise role/offer selection before increasing connection volume. Keep the channel secondary until it earns more time through qualified conversations.
