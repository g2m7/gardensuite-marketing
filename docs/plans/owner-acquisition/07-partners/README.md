# Service partners, brokers and independent advisers

Priority: P2 | Owner: Kaushik | Estimate: three exploratory conversations over weeks 3-4

## Goal

Find people with real estate relationships who can make a willing introduction or support a joint demonstration. Measure introduced estates and sales, not the number of business cards collected.

## Candidate order

1. Suppliers recommended by current customers: garden computer/network support and weighing-equipment service businesses.
2. Respected retired managers who now consult independently and can reach estate owners.
3. Tea brokers and advisers with established producer relationships.
4. Other relevant service firms only when a customer or public business record supports the relationship.

The [Guwahati Tea Auction Centre broker directory](https://www.assamteaxchange.com/brokers.aspx) is a public research starting point. It lists firms including Assam Tea Brokers, Contemporary Brokers and J Thomas. No willingness to promote GardenSuite is established by a directory listing.

## Setup

1. Ask two current customers which independent service providers they trust. Verify the business and its actual estate work.
2. Shortlist three candidates and show the working attendance/reporting package.
3. Ask for one permission-based introduction as a trial. Do not purchase their client list.
4. Prepare a one-page partner brief: estate fit, product scope, demonstration link, implementation responsibility and contact route.
5. If a paid arrangement is appropriate, document lead attribution, consent, payment trigger, amount, expiry and who supports the client. Do not promise exclusivity or a retainer before evidence.
6. Check introduced accounts against current sales activity and suppression before follow-up. Prevent two partners claiming the same estate without review.

## Partner approach draft

We build GardenSuite at Sarbani Associates for tea-garden attendance and daily reporting. I would like to show you the smaller attendance package and see whether it fits a need among estates you already serve. If there is a fit, we could start with one introduction where the estate wants to speak with us.

## Do

Keep commissions transparent between independent businesses. Pay for an agreed commercial outcome, such as a paid installation, under documented terms. Retain responsibility for truthful scope, training and support.

## Do not

Pay an estate employee secretly for influence, ask for confidential client lists, imply a broker endorses you without agreement, promise unsupported integrations, or hire an introducer only because they claim to know many owners.

## Goals and acceptance criteria

- [ ] Three candidates have evidence of relevant estate relationships.
- [ ] Each understands the package and who delivers/supports it.
- [ ] At least one mutually accepted estate introduction sought within thirty days of partner testing.
- [ ] Any commercial terms are recorded before a payment commitment.
- [ ] Source, estate, permission and conversion are tracked.

Review after thirty days. Continue candidates making useful introductions; end open-ended activity with no concrete estate conversation. Add partners only when the sales and implementation team can serve the leads.
