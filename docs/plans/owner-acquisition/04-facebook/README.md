# Facebook groups and founder content

Priority: P1 after the micro magnet works | Owner: Kaushik | Estimate: 60-90 minutes weekly plus replies

## Goal

Reach working tea-estate owners and invite a private, voluntary response to the owner check. Treat group audience quality as something to verify, not an assumption based on the word tea.

## Setup

1. Use Kaushik's real profile and a GardenSuite page with Sarbani Associates, website, role and a clear business identity. Do not create a fake planter persona.
2. Research five to eight groups; choose two or three with recent operational conversations and identifiable estate owners/directors. Search terms include Assam tea planters, tea estate owners, Dibrugarh tea industry and Tinsukia tea gardens.
3. Record group URL, rules, observed audience, recent activity, posting permission and last touch in the channel register. Exclude consumer tea, tourism, job-only and inactive groups from the primary test.
4. Build a short post and sample report visual for each group. Link to the same magnet with a group-specific source tag that contains no member identity.
5. Read existing discussions and make useful, relevant contributions. Publish only within the rules; ask the administrator where required.

## Posting routine

One helpful, separately written post per qualified group per week is a working maximum, not a platform entitlement. Two founder posts weekly can test different owner questions. Reply to real questions and note which ideas resonate. Do not paste the same promotional text into every group.

Start with the [micro-magnet content](../03-owner-micro-magnet/CONTENT.md) adapted to the discussion. Two hooks to test sequentially:

- You receive the daily hazira total. Can you see what changed before it reached you?
- When you are away from the garden, can you tell whether the attendance report is complete?

Do not split tiny audiences into multiple simultaneous statistical tests. Compare owner responses over successive posts and record the exposure conditions.

## First post draft

For tea garden owners who review the garden from another location:

Does your daily hazira report show only the total, or can you also check worker details, corrections and records still waiting to arrive?

We have prepared a short owner check and a one-page report format. Use them with your current reporting process.

Take the check privately: [published magnet link]

GardenSuite by Sarbani Associates.

This draft must be adapted to the specific group and linked only after the page is live. A public comment or phone number is not required to use it.

## Public-to-private path

Post -> private website check -> useful result -> optional resource email -> separately requested product conversation.

A person can participate without commenting their role or estate publicly. If someone asks publicly for details, answer the question and ask before a private sales message. Do not extract all group members into a contact database or cold-message commenters.

## Do

Disclose your connection to GardenSuite, use sample records, explain one practical check, and let people click privately. Track the estate after voluntary contact. Remove or amend a post if the rules/admin require it.

## Do not

Accuse managers, invent a client result, use fake engagement, post duplicate promotions, ask for mobile numbers in comments, or treat group membership as purchase intent. Do not promise an ad audience made from the membership of a third-party group. Paid work has a separate plan and capability check.

## Goals and acceptance criteria

- [ ] Two or three relevant groups documented with rules and evidence of the right audience.
- [ ] Each post has a distinct source tag and a relevant contribution to that group.
- [ ] The link, resource delivery and requested follow-up work on a phone.
- [ ] Weekly reporting distinguishes visits, completed checks, verified owner accounts and demo requests.
- Initial activity target: two group-specific posts weekly across selected groups, plus two founder posts, within actual group rules.
- Outcome goal: contribute toward the shared ten-owner/three-demo-conversation social test; do not count every group toward a separate duplicated target.

After four weeks, continue groups producing relevant conversations. Stop groups with repeated removal, administrator objection or consistently unrelated respondents. If reach is too small, report the distribution constraint and consider a capped paid reach test only after validating the offer.
