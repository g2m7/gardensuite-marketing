# Cold email pilot

Priority: P1 | Owner: Kaushik | Scope: 20 independent/mid-tier estate accounts in Dibrugarh and Tinsukia | Status: Live pilot

## Goal

Test whether a short attendance and daily-report offer creates relevant buyer replies. Twenty is an experiment size. It is not a technical limit imposed by Gmail or proof of safe delivery.

## Decision

The owner authorized live sending on 5 September 2026. The first cleared subset contains three estates and was activated in Snov that evening. Continue with no more than four newly cleared estates per working day. This is a maximum, not a quota. Each address must pass every account, identity, estate-association, suppression and Snov Valid check before it is added.

The live campaign evidence, exact copy and sending controls are recorded in [Launch record](../../../../marketing/outreach/sep-2026-pilot/LAUNCH_2026-09-05.md).

Spacing cannot guarantee inbox placement. Sender authentication, recipient relevance, mailbox quality, provider rules and recipient responses still matter. Read [Audit](AUDIT.md) for the evidence and the owner's existing provider-risk decision.

## Setup order

1. Use the current research CSV and internal history source. Preserve the current-client, active-negotiation, rejection and do-not-contact exclusions.
2. Find one current owner/director or other qualifying buyer per estate. Owners/directors take priority; use a manager only where they are a useful willing contact with an understood buying route.
3. Verify email association and Snov Valid status. A published address can be useful evidence but does not establish willingness to receive a pitch.
4. Prepare the short [Sequence](SEQUENCE.md), with exact scope verified by the product lead.
5. Follow [Setup](SETUP.md), including delivered-message authentication and reply-stop testing.
6. Preview a concrete launch packet with cleared recipients, messages and settings. Activate only with the user's sending authorization.
7. Add no more than four new approved contacts on a sending day. Review replies before sending the next group.

A first batch can consist of fewer than four eligible accounts. Do not wait for five backups or an exact worker count if a credible operating-scale proxy already satisfies the current rule. Do not waive identity, estate association, exclusion or Snov validation.

## Goals

- Twenty first contacts over five sending days when enough qualified accounts are ready.
- At least two positive relevant replies OR one qualified demonstration after all 20 complete or exit the sequence.
- Zero duplicate, suppressed or wrongly associated recipients.
- Record actual sends, rejections, opt-outs, replies, demos and sales. Server acceptance is not evidence of Inbox placement.

## Do

Use the full mailbox kaushik@getgardensuite.in and the truthful sender identity. Use one short sequence; insert only verified name and estate. Honour replies and stop requests before the next automated step. Reply to interest within one working day as an internal target.

## Do not

Attach a brochure to the first cold email, invent a nearby reference, use deceptive Re: subjects, guess email patterns, purchase owner mobile lists, rotate domains to evade blocks, or start WhatsApp after silence. No mandatory elaborate estate-specific opening line. No indefinite warming or arbitrary additional domain-age wait after functional checks pass.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Each imported recipient in the first subset passes current account/contact/verification/suppression checks.
- [x] Live copy makes a true standalone offer and does not depend on reference permission.
- [ ] Delivered test authentication passes and a test reply exits the sequence.
- [x] Per-day new contacts, automated traffic and manual replies fit the schedule and limits.
- [x] The launch packet is authorized and the first subset is active; research automation remains unable to send.
- [ ] Results are reviewed after the last follow-up, rather than declaring failure after the first five days.

If fewer than 20 addresses can be cleared, launch the authorized cleared subset and continue research. Report the actual denominator; do not manufacture a full list.
