# GardenSuite owner acquisition plan

Version: 1 | Prepared: 2026-09-05 | Owner: Kaushik Majumder, Sarbani Associates

Status: Complete planning and setup specification. Public assets, lead capture, campaigns and spending are not activated by these files. The latest user direction and [CURRENT_STRATEGY.md](../../../marketing/outreach/CURRENT_STRATEGY.md) govern operations.

## Goal

Sell a useful standalone mobile attendance and daily-report package, establish successful use at the estate, then offer relevant ERP modules at a larger ticket size. Acquire voluntary conversations with estate owners and directors without depending on their private mobile numbers or a manager introducing the product.

## What changed after the owner's feedback

- Referrals already produce sales. Plan around one introduction from a willing customer, not an assumed two or more.
- Association circulation of long marketing emails has failed. It is a low-priority experiment with a firm effort limit.
- The owner reports resistance from some managers to independent checks. Direct owner acquisition takes priority; do not turn that experience into a public accusation about all managers.
- The owner reports conversion above 50%, including inbound or cold outreach. Sample size, denominator, source split and standalone-package conversion remain unknown. It is not a forecast for new channels.
- A small social micro magnet should give value before requesting contact information. Download permission and sales follow-up permission are distinct.
- Twenty initial cold emails over five working days is the recommended pilot: four new estates per day. It is a volume choice, not a promise to avoid spam.

## Roadmap

### 5-9 September: prepare

1. Confirm package scope and price. Build the owner report example with sample data.
2. Complete email research and Snov validation; prepare the first cleared recipients, final copy and Snov settings.
3. Draft the micro magnet, test it with three existing owner customers, and implement the result/capture/permission path.
4. Set up the restricted operations tracker and source labels. Test delivery, opt-out and reply handling.
5. Pause the already-authorized warm-up on 9 September, as previously instructed. This document does not itself change Snov.

### 10-16 September: launch the first tests

Send up to four new estate emails on 10, 11, 14, 15 and 16 September. Never fill a quota with an unverified address. The dates move forward if readiness slips; do not compress the missed volume.

Publish the owner magnet only after its release checklist passes. Begin with two or three qualified Facebook groups and founder posts. Continue requested referral introductions. Reply to inbound interest within one working day as an internal target.

### Weeks 3-4

Review email replies and social estate quality. Test LinkedIn organically. Hold three partner conversations. Improve one weak point in the magnet before considering paid promotion. Reserve time for demos and installations.

### Days 31-60

Repeat only channels that produced qualified conversations. If reach is the constraint and the offer was useful to owners, run one capped Meta experiment. Test a small set of letters or combine invited garden visits with existing service travel. Review first installations before seeking expansion.

### Days 61-90

Choose the two most productive sources using cost, founder time, paid sales and support burden. Document one permitted customer story. Offer the next ERP module only when the estate has a clear operating need and the attendance installation is working.

### Months 4-12

Expand one geography or partner route at a time after service capacity and unit economics support it. Add search content and paid discovery where measured demand exists. Hire or contract research/operations help when response or installation capacity is the bottleneck. No funding round or hiring budget is assumed.

## Capacity and budget

Use an initial planning allowance of six hours per week for acquisition operations, excluding demos, installation and asset development: two hours email/research review, two hours content and social replies, one hour referrals/partners, one hour metrics. No paid media, paid tooling, referral fee or retainer is authorized by this plan. The amount is an open decision, not a reason to delay organic preparation. [Decisions](DECISIONS.md) defines the needed numbers and [Operations](12-operations/README.md) defines cost measurement.

## Global rules

- Make the estate the account; link people, permissions and touches to it.
- Preserve every do-not-contact decision across channels. A new source is not permission to restart contact.
- Give the resource requested before offering a sale. Do not require a public comment or private mobile number to access it.
- Use simple tea-garden language. No em dashes, accusations, invented savings, fake testimonials or guaranteed inbox placement.
- Say face attendance helps stop proxy attendance. Explain offline capture and later sync; do not imply every MIS value is live.
- Show Sarbani Associates clearly. Use client names or operational data only within the client's permission.
- Automation can prepare records, draft assets and validate setup. External sends, publishing and spending require the user's authorization for the concrete action. Do not add repeated approval gates to already-authorized preparation.
- Keep secrets and prospect personal details out of public plans, URLs, analytics and screenshots.
