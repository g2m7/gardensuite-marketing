## Go/no-go and rollback

### Proposed direct, referral and organic gate, 2026-09-30

- [ ] Offer, product, privacy, tracking, forms, CRM, assets, sales and implementation checks pass.
- [ ] Named campaign owner accepts the direct launch.
- [ ] Stop owner and rollback steps are tested.
- [ ] Direct launch is `GO` or `NO-GO` with reason and timestamp.
- [ ] If `NO-GO`, affected actions stay off and safe preparation continues.

### Proposed paid checkpoint, 2026-10-05

- [ ] Direct funnel end-to-end QA passes in a non-production destination.
- [ ] Paid economics, geography, caps, billing, access, creative, keyword, audience and stop controls pass.
- [ ] Owner gives explicit paid activation approval.
- [ ] Paid launch is `GO` or `NO-GO` with campaign IDs, maximum spend and timestamp.
- [ ] If `NO-GO`, spend remains zero.

### Production biometric gate, before any trial activation

- [ ] Privacy and security owner signs the data process.
- [ ] Estate and worker notice or approved basis is ready.
- [ ] Access, export, deletion, retention and incident route pass.
- [ ] Test uses approved or fictional data.
- [ ] Biometric activation is `GO` or `NO-GO` with timestamp and evidence.
- [ ] If `NO-GO`, use fictional demo data and do not collect production faces.

### Rollback test

- [ ] Stop owner can pause the page, form, ad, organic post, referral ask or trial intake.
- [ ] Suppression and DNC remain in force after rollback.
- [ ] A failed or duplicate request is reconciled in the private tracker.
- [ ] Evidence and timestamps are preserved.
- [ ] Restart needs a new named approval and QA record.
