## Offer decision

The proposed public offer window is 2026-10-01 00:00 IST through 2026-11-30 23:59 IST. `Request Puja Trial` is a proposed campaign CTA pending approval. The current `Book Free Demo` and `Email Us` actions remain available. A trial request asks for a short conversation, not an immediate purchase.

A trial starts on the estate's agreed activation date and runs for 30 days. The written agreement, activation date, data process and named support owner control the clock. Ending a public offer does not stop a signed trial or its agreed close-out.

## Proposed eligibility

An estate can request a trial when it:

- operates a tea garden or a clearly defined division or worker group;
- has a current office contact and an operational owner who can approve the scope;
- can use an approved Android attendance device and the agreed field workflow;
- accepts the trial data agreement and privacy notice;
- passes the capacity and technical-fit check;
- has a named activation date and support contact.

A consumer, a small office seeking self-service attendance, or a prospect seeking instant sign-up is a poor fit. An inbound enquiry from another supported tea region can be assessed, but it is not added to the September cold list.

Current clients receive account support or an expansion discussion, not a new net-new trial unless the campaign owner approves a separate scope.

## What is excluded or separately quoted

- Android device, camera equipment, scale or other hardware.
- Travel, on-site training and expenses.
- Internet, electricity, connectivity or a site network.
- Data cleanup, duplicate master correction or non-standard worker-master preparation.
- Custom integrations, custom reports, APIs or work with an unverified third-party system.
- Payroll, factory, stores and full ERP unless a separate signed module scope says so.
- Daily MIS until the exact reports and inputs are technically confirmed and included in the signed scope.
- Smart weighing unless the estate and product owner approve it as an optional module.
- A full ERP commitment or an automatic annual renewal.

The internal [pricing structure](../../pricing/PRICING_STRUCTURE.md) lists existing tiers and hardware prices. It does not define this trial's worker/device mapping. Do not publish a trial price or imply that 100 workers fit a tier until the product owner signs that relationship.

## Trial lifecycle

### 1. Enquiry and qualification

1. Capture the estate name, region, decision role, worker group size, current attendance process, device question, preferred contact and consent choice.
2. Check the estate account, prior activity, DNC and current-client status before any response or referral.
3. Confirm a decision-maker role, a useful attendance problem and support fit. A form fill alone is not a qualified trial.
4. Answer within the approved sales SLA. Offer a demo or technical fit call.

### 2. Demo and written scope

1. Show face verification, hazira context, field capture, later sync and office review with fictional or permitted sample data.
2. Treat GS Face plus office review as the proposed core. Mark daily MIS as conditional on exact report and input verification. Mark smart weighing as optional and full ERP as later expansion.
3. Confirm the one-division or worker-group boundary, user count, device, activation date, support owner and data plan.
4. Send a plain-language trial agreement. The customer must understand the free items, exclusions, 30-day clock, exit choices and no auto-renewal.

### 3. Activation

1. Privacy and security owner signs the data process before production face enrolment.
2. Implementation lead confirms capacity, device compatibility, master-data inputs, office review steps and escalation route.
3. Enrol only the agreed worker group. Do not collect faces in the marketing form or in unapproved screenshots.
4. Run one remote training session and record attendance, questions and agreed fallback.
5. Send the estate the activation confirmation, support contact and first weekly review date.

### 4. Active trial

- Keep a weekly review of records captured, sync status, office review, corrections, open issues, worker readiness and next action.
- Use the trial scorecard. Do not promise an attendance, cost or accuracy result before the estate's own records are reviewed.
- Log support hours, unresolved issues, data exports and every scope change.
- A new worker group, device, user, module or integration requires a new decision and possibly a new agreement.

### 5. Day-30 decision

Before the trial ends, the sales and implementation owners choose one documented path:

- **Paid continuation:** Agree a paid scope, price, start date and support owner in writing. Do not assume the trial renews.
- **Export and exit:** Export the agreed standard data, confirm estate receipt, then delete or retain data as the agreement states.
- **Short documented extension:** Only with a named reason, new end date, capacity check and written approval.
- **No continuation:** Record the reason, export or deletion action and the date. No repeated sales pressure.

## Qualification rules

### In-scope

- Tea estate, division or worker group with a real attendance need.
- Owner, director, general manager, estate manager or garden manager can approve.
- Face attendance, hazira, field capture and office review are understood and technically possible.
- Device, connectivity, data and training responsibilities are named.

### Hold for review

- Existing system or a second vendor. Ask what it covers and what the estate wants to change. Do not attack the current vendor.
- More than 100 workers, multiple devices, multiple divisions or a custom integration.
- Worker unions, privacy questions, unclear data ownership or a request to bypass the review.
- A buyer wants full ERP before testing attendance.
- An inbound estate outside current service capacity. Record the region and seek a fit decision.

### Disqualify or do not contact

- Consumer or non-tea office request without a product-fit decision.
- Explicit DNC, stop, complaint, hard bounce or invalid contact.
- A request to collect production faces before privacy/security approval.
- A request for a claim, testimonial, client name or result that GardenSuite cannot evidence.

## Privacy and security gate

No production worker face data is collected until the privacy and security owner signs the review. The gate covers:

- purpose and notice to workers and estate;
- consent or other approved lawful basis and withdrawal process;
- collection, encryption, storage, access, logs and backups;
- role-based supervisor and office access;
- export, deletion and retention at day 30;
- incident response and stop authority;
- processor and vendor responsibilities;
- test data that contains no real worker face;
- worker and estate questions recorded for resolution.

If the gate fails, offer a demo using fictional or approved sample data and do not activate a production trial. Do not work around the gate by moving face images into a form, spreadsheet, email or analytics tool.

## Sales copy guardrails

Use:

- `GS Face helps stop proxy attendance.`
- `Field records can be captured offline and synced later, subject to the agreed setup.`
- `The office reviews the agreed attendance records before they are used.`
- `Remote setup, one training session and one standard export are included in the proposed trial scope.`
- `There is no auto-renewal and no full ERP commitment.`
- `Many estates keep software details private.`

Do not use:

- unverified accuracy or identification claims;
- fixed savings, time, cost or recovery figures;
- seasonal-condition, connectivity or device claims that were not tested;
- full-ERP, payroll, weighing or MIS claims without a signed scope;
- names, testimonials, screenshots or operational data without permission;
- language that promises a result the estate has not measured.

The phrase "free trial" must sit beside the scope, exclusions and data agreement. Do not bury the 30-day boundary in a long form.

## Stop rules

Pause the offer or a specific trial when:

- product, privacy, security or implementation evidence is missing;
- a worker or estate reports an unhandled privacy or data-access concern;
- a captured record, claim or proof point is misleading;
- a production face appears in an unapproved system or public asset;
- the five-trial capacity or an approved support limit is exceeded;
- the form, CRM or acknowledgement path loses a lead;
- the estate wants a scope or data change that has not been approved;
- the offer close or stop owner directs a pause.

The stop owner records the affected scope, time, reason, action and restart evidence. A blocked trial does not silently move to paid service or a different account.
