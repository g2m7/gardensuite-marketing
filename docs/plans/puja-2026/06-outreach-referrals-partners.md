## Boundary with the September pilot

The [current outreach strategy](../../../marketing/outreach/CURRENT_STRATEGY.md) remains the authority for live outreach. The September cold-email experiment stays within its current approved account, contact, validation, suppression, geography, sender, schedule and owner rules. This campaign does not:

- add a cold-email recipient or district;
- broaden the September email list;
- start a cold WhatsApp sequence;
- reactivate archived Brevo, WhatsApp or other legacy workflows;
- treat a Facebook group, LinkedIn group or downloaded list as a permission basis;
- override a DNC, rejection, current-client or active-negotiation exclusion.

A relevant inbound enquiry can come from another supported tea region. It becomes an estate account with a new source and service-fit review. It is not silently added to the September cold pilot.

## Account-based contact rule

The estate is the account. One estate may have several contact points, but one activity history and one DNC state. Before any contact, check:

1. current client or support account;
2. active sales discussion and assigned owner;
3. explicit prior rejection, stop or DNC;
4. district and current September pilot boundary;
5. estate fit, role, source and permission;
6. current contact verification date and source;
7. next action, owner and date.

A valid phone number, a form fill or a role dropdown does not equal permission to message. A self-declared owner remains unverified until the estate relationship is checked.

## Existing-client referral ask

Prepare one short ask for a willing existing client. Keep it human and respectful. It must not promise a discount, case study, client name or special result.

```text
Subject: A GS Face trial for another estate

Hello [verified name],

If you know an estate owner who is checking hazira from the field and wants a clearer office review, would you be comfortable making one introduction?

We are preparing a proposed 30-day assisted GS Face trial for a small division or worker group, subject to approval. We will check fit, data handling and support before any face data is collected.

There is no obligation. A reply of “not now” or “do not contact” will stop the ask.

With thanks,
[verified sender]
Sarbani Associates
```

Before sending any approved version, record the client's permission to be asked, date, channel, exact version and outcome. One introduction is the planning expectation. Do not build a forecast on multiple introductions.

### Referral handoff

1. Record the client as the referrer and the referred estate as a new account. Do not merge the two estates.
2. Ask the client to share only the estate name or business contact the estate expects. Do not ask the client to forward worker data.
3. Check current-client, active-sales, rejection and DNC status.
4. Send a neutral introduction or let the client make it. Do not pretend the referred estate asked for a trial.
5. Ask the estate about its attendance need, role, device, privacy process and support fit.
6. Offer current `Book Free Demo` and `Email Us` actions, plus `Request Puja Trial` only if that proposed CTA is approved.
7. Record source `referral`, the referrer ID in the private tracker and the next owner.

## LinkedIn route

LinkedIn is a later selective test under the existing [LinkedIn playbook](../../../marketing/social-media/linkedin-playbook.md). It may be used to find current owners, directors and managers who have made a public professional connection, but public availability is not a blanket marketing permission. Check the current account, group and platform rules before acting.

Prepare:

- a profile checklist for Sarbani Associates and GardenSuite;
- a short connection note that asks whether the person handles estate attendance;
- a follow-up that offers a practical view of face-verified hazira and office review;
- a separate approved message for a person who replies or requests a demo;
- a suppression and DNC check before every message;
- a rule against connecting to current clients, active negotiations or rejected estates.

Do not automate connection requests, scrape group members or send the same message widely. A positive reply moves the account to the sales owner. Silence is not permission to keep messaging.

## Permission-based WhatsApp route

WhatsApp is allowed only after:

- a positive email reply;
- an inbound enquiry;
- a documented request for WhatsApp;
- an existing business relationship that permits it and has been checked;
- a voluntary referral introduction where the person agrees.

Before sending, record the exact notice or request, purpose, source, time, contact and withdrawal route. Do not treat a website form, video view, ad click or group comment as WhatsApp permission. A stop request applies to WhatsApp and the matching phone record.

Prepare a short response, not a new sequence:

```text
Hello [verified name],

Thank you for contacting GardenSuite. We can show how GS Face helps stop proxy attendance by checking a worker before hazira is recorded, with a field-to-office review.

Would you prefer a short demo, an email with the trial scope, or a later call?

[verified sender]
Sarbani Associates
```

Do not send this draft without the sales owner's approval and the current outreach rules.

## Partner route

Partners are optional and lower priority. Suitable partners may include local IT service firms, estate-system advisers, device installers or consultants who already serve tea estates. A partner is not a source of unrestricted contacts.

Prepare a partner discussion pack:

- one-page GS Face workflow with fictional data;
- proposed trial boundary and exclusions;
- referral permission and DNC responsibilities;
- implementation capacity and support handoff;
- Sarbani Associates ownership and contact route;
- no client names or data without permission.

Do not pay a referral fee, share a prospect list, upload contacts or activate a partner campaign without a written agreement and owner approval. One suitable conversation is a test, not a growth assumption.

## Seasonal message rules

The Puja window is a timing reason, not a claim about estate results.

- Do not say seasonal conditions, harvest volume, savings or attendance will change because of Puja.
- Do not use religious or cultural pressure to request a reply.
- Use a practical invitation: review a small worker group during the offer window.
- If a 30-day trial is approved, tie its start to the agreed activation date, not to a festival date.
- Do not imply a discount or renewal that is not in the signed offer.
- Do not send a festival greeting as a substitute for a relevant product conversation.
- Keep the message short and use tea-garden words such as hazira, section, muster roll and office review.

## DNC and suppression

The master suppression register is checked before every import and every channel touch. It must record:

- normalized email and phone;
- estate account and contact;
- reason and source;
- date and person applying it;
- channel scope, if the decision is channel-specific;
- withdrawal or later review note.

Immediate suppress:

- explicit do-not-contact or stop;
- unsubscribe or opt-out;
- hard bounce or invalid address;
- spam complaint;
- wrong-person report that makes the address unsafe;
- a privacy or security request.

An invalid address stops that address. It does not automatically mark the whole estate uninterested. A current-client or active-negotiation exclusion overrides every acquisition route. A new file must never reactivate a suppressed record.

## Stop rules

Stop the affected outreach or referral route when:

- a recipient is a current client, active negotiation, prior rejection or DNC record;
- a contact's role, estate relationship or current address cannot be verified;
- a hard bounce, complaint, stop or wrong-person report appears;
- a message makes an unsupported claim or asks for worker face data;
- a person does not reply and the next planned message exceeds the approved sequence;
- a public group member is asked for a private number without a voluntary request;
- a partner asks for an unrestricted list, fee or public client reference;
- the owner or campaign owner has not approved the concrete send.

The outreach owner records account ID, channel, message version, time, stop reason and next action. Do not restart by importing the same address into a new campaign.
