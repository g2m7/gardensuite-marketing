# Campaign rationalization recommendation

Dated: 2026-09-24
Owner: GardenSuite campaign owner, with Sarbani Associates as product, sales and delivery owner
Status: Recommendation pending owner adoption. This document does not change live campaign authority or authorize an external action.

> **Continue, but consolidate.**

Keep the direction. Put the October-November work behind one GS Face acquisition campaign, finish the approved September email pilot, and treat product strategy and channel operations as separate layers.

## Verdict

Too many product strategies, campaigns and channel operations are being treated as equal campaigns. That makes the team choose between messages instead of running one agreed path to an estate trial.

The recommended decision is to continue with one acquisition campaign:

- **Working name:** `GardenSuite GS Face Puja Proof Campaign`
- **Proposed window:** October-November 2026, subject to owner approval
- **Promise:** `See whether GS Face can work for your estate's attendance before committing to a full rollout.`
- **Primary outcome:** A verified tea estate starts an approved, bounded GS Face trial.

The exact trial duration, worker limit, device count, free items, dates, CTA, data terms and support promise remain provisional until the named product, implementation, campaign, privacy and security owners approve them.

## Separate the three layers

| Layer | Job | October-November place | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product strategy | Decide where GardenSuite should go and what it should prove long term | Keep Tea Operations Control System as the long-term product direction | Labour control, workforce planning, daily MIS, smart weighing and the Digital Leaf Passport |
| Acquisition campaign | Win a defined estate result within a fixed window | Run one GS Face Puja proof campaign from assessment to bounded trial | One audience, offer, promise, tracker and scorecard |
| Channel operations | Move messages and people between campaign stages | Operate as channels under the campaign, not as rival campaigns | Approved September email, SEO, Facebook organic, referrals and named-account discovery |

A channel may have its own rules and review cadence. It should still point to the same campaign result.

## One north-star campaign

### Campaign promise

`See whether GS Face can work for your estate's attendance before committing to a full rollout.`

### Primary audience

Tea estate owners, directors, general managers, estate managers and garden managers who can approve a bounded attendance trial. Field supervisors and office users remain important implementation stakeholders, but the acquisition conversation starts with the estate decision role.

### Primary result

A verified estate account agrees the scope, start date, data terms and review method, then starts the approved GS Face trial. Form fills, video views and broad reach are supporting measures, not the campaign result.

### Offer ladder

| Step | Offer | Purpose | Boundary |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 15-minute workflow assessment or demo | Show the field-to-office attendance workflow and find service fit | No production face-data collection |
| 2 | Bounded assisted GS Face trial | Let the estate judge use in a limited real workflow | Duration, scope, capacity, data and exit terms need written approval |
| 3 | Paid GS Face rollout | Continue the attendance product after a successful trial | Quote only the approved estate scope |
| 4 | Verified adjacent module such as daily MIS or smart weighing | Add a module with a confirmed workflow and estate need | Do not promise live reports or scale integration before technical confirmation |
| 5 | Tea Loss Audit or broader Tea Operations Control System | Expand into a measured operational problem | Use as phase two, after the first product works and proof exists |

### Campaign operating rule

All active work must name the same:

1. audience;
2. entry product, GS Face;
3. offer, the approved proof path from assessment to bounded trial;
4. estate-account tracker; and
5. result, an approved trial that starts.

## One scorecard

The scorecard should be the [Puja campaign scorecard](templates/campaign-scorecard.csv), filtered to the single Puja campaign and its supported source values. It should not create separate channel scorecards with competing success claims.

| Stage or quality check | One measure | Required evidence | Proposed planning benchmark |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Account quality | Distinct verified estate accounts with fit and decision-role review | Account record, source, validation and exclusion checks | Evidence, no fixed count |
| Assessment or demo | Completed 15-minute workflow assessment or demo | Demo record with estate, role, need, outcome and next date | 6 |
| Trial acceptance | Approved bounded trial with scope and start date | Signed or approved trial record | 3 |
| Trial start | Trial actually starts within capacity | Activation record and data approval | At least 1 |
| Paid continuation | At least one paid GS Face continuation | Payment and activation evidence | 1 |
| Data and operations | Suppression failures, privacy incidents, response misses and support effort | Incident log, SLA record, support time and close-out record | 0 suppression or privacy failures |
| Expansion | Adjacent module or later diagnostic opened from a verified estate need | Qualified need and written next scope | Count, not a launch target |

The 12 verified enquiries, 6 qualified demos, 3 accepted trials and 1 paid continuation figures are provisional planning goals in the [Puja package](README.md). They are not forecasts. They need owner adoption before they become campaign targets.

Count distinct estates, not form fills. Review the scorecard weekly once the campaign is approved. Review delivery, complaints and response capacity daily during any active send.

## Approval gates

### Portfolio adoption

1. The owner adopts or changes the single-campaign direction.
2. The owner names the campaign owner, stop owner and backups.
3. The decision is added to the current outreach authority if it changes outreach, sending, spend, CRM or public activation.

### Direct, referral and organic activation

The existing [Puja decisions](DECISIONS.md) gate must pass:

- approved public product scope and wording;
- approved form, consent, storage and trial-data process;
- confirmed implementation capacity and support owner;
- accepted sales response process;
- one approved estate-account tracker and suppression check;
- non-production form, routing, attribution and no-personal-data-in-URLs tests;
- approved creative claims, permissions and trust copy.

### Production trial activation

Before any worker face data is collected:

- privacy and security owners approve the trial-data process;
- product verifies the GS Face build and fallback workflow;
- implementation approves the estate scope and support plan;
- the estate signs the trial and data agreement.

### Paid activation

Paid media also needs:

- approved geography and cell split;
- approved total and per-channel caps;
- approved billing owner and account access;
- recorded trial cost, gross margin, maximum acceptable acquisition cost and maximum test loss;
- end-to-end tracking QA;
- explicit owner approval for platform write, activation and spend.

### PR and broad expansion

PR stays paused until a permitted pilot result or real story exists. Tea Loss Audit and broader Tea Operations Control System expansion stay outside the current headline until the GS Face step produces the evidence and owner approval to introduce them.

## Explicit non-authorizations

This memo and the accompanying deck do not authorize:

- a new or changed live campaign;
- a new send or new recipient;
- wider cold-email geography;
- cold WhatsApp;
- publication of a landing page, post, ad, press pitch or offer;
- a Meta, Search, retargeting or partner-spend budget;
- a live CRM or tracker change;
- production biometric enrolment or worker face-data collection;
- a final trial duration, scope, free-item list, capacity number or data term;
- a public client name, reference, quotation, saving or performance claim;
- full ERP promotion in the Puja acquisition campaign.

The [current outreach strategy](../../../marketing/outreach/CURRENT_STRATEGY.md) and [Puja decisions](DECISIONS.md) remain authoritative until the owner adopts a dated change.
