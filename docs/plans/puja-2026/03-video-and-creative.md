## Asset aim

Show one clear field-to-office journey: a worker face is checked before hazira is recorded, the record is captured in the field, synced later when connectivity returns, and reviewed by the office. Do not build the video around an unverified attendance result, saving, payroll total or ERP outcome.

The proposed CTA is `Request Puja Trial`. Secondary text may say `Book Free Demo`. Offer terms must point to the approved page and use the 30-day scope from [the offer plan](01-offer-and-free-trial.md).

## Case-study template

Do not publish a case study until the estate gives written permission. Keep this structure ready for a permitted story:

```text
# [Estate or region] and GS Face

## Before
[What the estate agreed to describe. No unsupported accusation.]

## What was tested
[Division or worker group, dates, device and agreed workflow.]

## What the team used
[GS Face steps shown, with fictional or approved records.]

## What the estate reviewed
[Actual, documented observation. No invented percentage or saving.]

## What was not included
[Hardware, travel, custom work or modules outside the scope.]

## Permission and privacy
[Publication channels, dates, approved name or anonymous region, data removed.]

## Contact route
[Request Puja Trial or Book Free Demo. Sarbani Associates ownership.]
```

A case study is a record of what happened at one estate. It is not a promise for every estate.

Record at the source resolution supported by the device. Do not upscale a low-quality screen recording. Check motion, text size and safe areas on a phone before approval.

## Captions and accessibility

- Caption every spoken line and important on-screen instruction.
- Use plain English first, then an approved regional-language version.
- Keep captions high contrast, within safe margins and readable without sound.
- Add a transcript, chapter list and text alternative to the website version.
- Describe visual actions in the transcript, including face check, sync status and office review.
- Do not use rapid flashes, sound-only proof or text too small to read on a phone.
- Check pronunciation of `hazira`, `GS Face`, `Sarbani Associates` and the estate region with a local reviewer.

## Claims, privacy and permission checks

Before approval, the creative owner records for every asset:

- asset ID and version;
- product build and date of capture;
- exact spoken and on-screen claim;
- source of each number, if any;
- whether the face or record is fictional, permitted or not used;
- data region, retention and deletion state;
- client or employee permission, rights holder and approved channels with dates;
- music, font, stock and third-party rights;
- reviewer, date, expiry or stop condition, restart evidence and `Ready`, `Blocked` or `Approved` status.

No public asset may contain a real worker face, client dashboard, private contact, payroll figure, savings figure, testimonial or unapproved result. A screenshot showing a match does not prove that every worker will match every time.

## Review workflow

1. Creative drafts using the product claim sheet and a fictional account.
2. Product owner checks the current build, terminology and workflow.
3. Privacy owner checks data, notice, consent, worker visibility and asset exposure.
4. Sales owner checks objection answers, trial scope and CTA.
5. Creative owner checks captions, audio, aspect ratios, file size and source rights.
6. Campaign owner records the approval, expiry or stop condition in the [asset register](templates/asset-register.csv).
7. Only the approved version is scheduled. A changed claim, screen or language sends the asset back to review.

- [ ] At least six objection clips have a source question and product-approved answer.

- [ ] No asset promises a result that was not verified or a full ERP inclusion.
- [ ] Stop owner can withdraw an asset from every scheduled placement.

## Stop rules

Stop a recording, edit, caption or publication when:

- a current product build cannot verify the shown behavior;
- a real worker face, client record or private data is visible;
- a client permission or third-party right is missing;
- a caption changes the scope or makes an unsupported claim;
- a product, privacy or sales reviewer rejects the asset;
- the file exposes notification, location, contact or account information;
- a platform reports a safety, copyright or tracking issue.

Record the asset ID, affected placements, timestamp, reason, owner and restart evidence. Do not reuse a rejected cut as a new asset without a new review.
