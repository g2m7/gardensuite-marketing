## Package and pricing reconciliation

[PRICING_STRUCTURE.md](../../pricing/PRICING_STRUCTURE.md) remains a current internal pricing reference dated May 2026. It describes annual software tiers, hardware, training and support. It does not match the proposed Puja trial boundary of up to 100 workers on one approved device.

Use this rule until the trial-duration, scope and free-item proposals are approved:

1. The trial does not quote a public annual price or a discounted annual price.
2. The sales team uses the written trial scope and end-of-trial agreement. It does not map 100 workers into an existing device tier without a product decision.
3. Hardware, scale, travel, connectivity, data cleanup and custom work remain separate.
4. The existing pricing file is not rewritten by this campaign plan. Product and campaign owners must record any approved package or price change in a dated quote or decision update.
5. Full ERP, payroll, factory, stores and later expansion are not included in a GS Face trial unless a separate signed scope says so.

The campaign must not publish a savings comparison or payback claim. Entry-package economics and acceptable acquisition cost remain an internal worksheet until actual cost and conversion data are available.

## Geography decision

- **Inbound:** Proposed handling is to accept and qualify from currently supported tea regions, subject to campaign and service-fit approval. Record whether capacity and current product support fit the estate.
- **Existing cold outreach:** Keep the September campaign limited to its current approved Dibrugarh/Tinsukia accounts and rules. This package does not authorize a new list or send.
- **Referrals:** An existing client may make a voluntary introduction. The referred estate still passes account, contact, suppression and service-fit checks.
- **Paid:** Prepare separate candidate cells for North Bengal and Dibrugarh/Tinsukia. Owner selects the actual cells. Do not infer a paid location from the inbound list.
- **Groups:** A third-party Facebook group's members cannot be claimed as a directly selected paid audience. Join only under group rules and use organic participation.

## Trial capacity decision

Five active trials is a provisional planning ceiling. The implementation lead must confirm:

- who schedules setup and training;
- who handles urgent support during the offer;
- the maximum worker-master import and review load;
- the time needed for weekly review;
- what happens when the ceiling is full.

The number can be removed from copy until confirmed. Paid spend must be paused when a verified qualified lead would exceed approved capacity. A trial is not accepted until the estate, scope, activation date and data agreement are recorded.

## CRM destination decision

The first campaign needs one estate account with linked contacts, source, permissions, suppression, activity, trial and scorecard records. A restricted controlled workbook is the provisional default because it matches the owner-acquisition operating plan. It must be private, backed up and owned by a named person. An existing private CRM may be used if it has equivalent fields and passes access and deletion tests.

This documentation tree stores templates only. It must never hold prospect PII, worker face data, access tokens or production exports.

## Biometric and trial-data decision

No production face data can be collected until the privacy and security owner signs the review. The review must cover purpose, notice, lawful basis or consent process agreed for the deployment, access roles, encryption and storage, retention, export, deletion, incident response, worker rights, vendor/subprocessor use, backups and test evidence.

The trial agreement must state what happens at day 30: export and estate-controlled copy, deletion by GardenSuite, or a documented retention basis and period. It must also state what happens to backups and access after exit. A public form must not accept worker face images or identity documents.

## Proof and permission decision

Proof may use approved repository screenshots and fictional sample data. Before publication, the creative owner records the product version, whether faces or estate data appear, the rights holder and the permitted channels and dates. A client name, quote, private reference, screen recording or case study needs separate written permission. One permission does not cover every use.

Default public trust line: `Many estates keep software details private.` Name Sarbani Associates as the company that builds and supports GardenSuite.
