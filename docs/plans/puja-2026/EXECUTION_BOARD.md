## Board rules

- Every lane starts preparation on 2026-09-24. A blocked external lane does not stop safe drafting or QA in another lane.
- `Approved` needs a person, date and evidence path. A planned date or a prepared file is not approval.
- An external action needs a clean global gate. The 2026-10-05 paid earliest date is a proposed checkpoint, not current authority. No platform write or spend occurs before the paid gate.
- Update one estate account per lead. Do not use raw form fills as campaign results.
- Keep privacy, product truth, claims, permission, tracking and spend checks visible.
- Pre-launch form and CRM tests use a non-production destination. If production is unavoidable, the campaign, web and privacy owners must approve it in writing, restrict access, use a clearly synthetic record, set deletion timing, preserve rollback evidence and exclude the record from campaign metrics.

## Stop and rollback ownership

The named stop owner can pause a page, form, ad, post, referral ask or trial intake when there is a privacy fault, misleading claim, broken capture, suppression failure, spend overrun, platform warning, product mismatch or capacity breach. The stop owner records the time, affected asset, reason, owner and recovery evidence. They do not silently restart an asset.

Required stop evidence: asset ID, channel, timestamp IST, issue, decision, suppression or refund action where relevant, and named approver for restart.
