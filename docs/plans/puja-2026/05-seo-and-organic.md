## Puja content briefs

### Campaign page

- Primary intent: face attendance for tea gardens.
- Offer: proposed 30-day assisted GS Face trial, with the signed scope.
- Proposed CTA: `Request Puja Trial`, pending approval. Preserve `Book Free Demo` and `Email Us` where required.
- Sections: problem, face-verified hazira, field capture, later sync, office review, proposed trial inclusions and exclusions, privacy, conditional daily MIS after exact report and input checks, optional smart weighing, trust by Sarbani Associates, FAQ and CTA.
- No fake price, rating, case study or unsupported savings.
- Follow the complete new-page checklist in [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md).

### Practical guide: hazira and office review

- Explain how a field record can be reviewed, corrected and exported, using a fictional or permitted example.
- Use hazira, section, work code, muster roll and office terms.
- Link to the face-attendance page, the Puja page and the report template where useful.
- Do not state an accuracy rate, time saving or cost reduction without evidence.

### FAQ and objection content

Prepare one useful answer per approved objection: offline field work, office review, device problem, existing software, trial scope, data at day 30 and no auto-renewal. Link each answer to the relevant product or campaign page. Keep the answer useful even if the reader does not buy.

### Regional content

Do not create a new page for every district or a near-copy of the campaign page. Update an existing regional page only when it has distinct, useful information for owners in that region. The page must identify the tea-growing area, the same verified GS Face workflow and relevant local contact route. It must not imply that the office has a named client or a local installation unless permission supports it.

## Existing page integration

Before changing a page, inventory the current H1, title, canonical, schema, CTA, links and product claim. Keep the change narrow:

1. Add one descriptive link from the homepage or relevant attendance page to the approved Puja page.
2. Add one link from the Puja page to the existing face-attendance page and one to the relevant adjacent MIS or resource page.
3. Update the attendance page only with product-approved trial, setup, sync and export wording.
4. Keep `Book Free Demo` and `Email Us` on pages where repository rules require them.
5. Add the page to the sitemap with a reviewed priority and change frequency.
6. Record old and new URLs, internal links, metadata, schema, image paths and test results.

Do not publish the page while the trial scope, exact daily MIS reports and inputs, privacy path or tracking route is unresolved. A draft can sit in a review queue with status `Blocked`.

## Regional language approach

- Start with English because it is the current page and product authority.
- Prepare Bengali, Assamese and Hindi variants only after a local reviewer checks the tea-garden terms, offer boundary and CTA.
- Keep the meaning consistent. Do not translate an unverified product claim into a stronger promise.
- Use familiar terms such as hazira, plucking, muster roll and section after local review.
- Put language version and reviewer in the asset register.
- Do not create automatic near-duplicate pages in each language. Use a useful page, a translated variant with canonical rules, or an accessible resource as appropriate.
- If a local reviewer is unavailable, keep the English source and mark the variant `Blocked` rather than guessing.

## Group conduct

- Identify the group rules before posting.
- Contribute an answer, checklist or report format relevant to the group.
- Use no manager-blaming language and no unsupported claim about the group's current software.
- Do not duplicate a promotional post across groups.
- Do not ask a public member for a private number or move a conversation to private without permission.
- Record the group, rule check, post version, date, response and any removal.
- If a member asks for a private conversation, route it through the estate account and permission record.

A group is not a paid audience. Do not state that its members can be directly selected for advertising.

## Internal linking

Every campaign page and guide should make the route clear:

```text
Homepage -> Puja trial page -> face-attendance page
Puja trial page -> relevant office/MIS or hazira resource
Hazira guide -> face-attendance page and Puja trial page
Regional page -> Puja trial page or relevant product page
```

Use descriptive anchors such as `Request Puja Trial for GS Face`, `See how face-verified hazira is recorded`, and `Review the office attendance workflow`. Do not use `click here`, keyword stuffing or links that point to a different offer.

## GSC, indexing and technical checks

At page publication and weekly review:

- confirm the URL returns the expected page and canonical;
- confirm the page is indexable unless a deliberate no-go decision is recorded;
- check sitemap inclusion, robots directives and internal links;
- inspect GSC URL inspection and indexing status without treating inspection as instant ranking;
- record page title, description, H1, schema, image alt and mobile rendering;
- check Search Console clicks, impressions, queries and average position against actual data, not a forecast;
- record 404s, redirect chains, duplicate titles, slow pages and mobile errors;
- check that the campaign source parameters join to the CRM without PII;
- preserve the six-query tracking set from the SEO authority and add campaign queries without changing its historical baseline.

## Stop rules

Stop or withdraw a page, post or asset when:

- the product owner cannot verify a workflow or technical statement;
- a privacy notice, consent request, link or form path fails;
- a client name, screenshot, quote or estate data lacks permission;
- a regional translation changes the offer or makes a stronger claim;
- a page has a broken canonical, sitemap entry, schema, image or mobile layout;
- a platform removes or warns on the content;
- the page asks for worker biometric data in a marketing path;
- a group rule is broken or a member's stop request is ignored.

Record the URL or asset ID, timestamp, issue, stop owner, correction and restart evidence. Keep the useful evergreen material separate from the time-limited Puja offer.
