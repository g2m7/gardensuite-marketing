## Objective

Build awareness for GS Face and start practical estate conversations during Puja. Lead with face-verified hazira and office review, subject to technical sign-off. The proposed core package is GS Face plus office review. Include daily MIS only after its exact reports and inputs are technically confirmed. Smart weighing is optional. Offer full ERP later, when the estate needs it and the first system is working.

## Offer at a glance

- Proposed public offer window: 2026-10-01 00:00 IST through 2026-11-30 23:59 IST.
- Proposed free offer: 30 days of assisted GS Face trial access from the estate's agreed activation date.
- Proposed trial boundary: one estate division or worker group, up to 100 workers, one approved Android attendance device and up to two supervisor or office users.
- Proposed free items during the trial: software access, remote setup, enrolment help, one training session, office review, one standard export and weekly review.
- Proposed exclusions or separate quotes: hardware, scales, travel, connectivity, data cleanup, custom integration and work outside the written trial scope.
- Proposed terms: no auto-renewal and no full ERP commitment.
- Proposed safety gate: no production worker face data until the biometric, privacy and security review passes.

The scope, free items and maximum of five concurrent trials remain provisional until product, privacy and implementation owners sign off. See [Offer and free trial](01-offer-and-free-trial.md).

## Internal outcome goals

These are planning goals, not forecasts. The reporting date is 2026-12-31.

| Goal | Count | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Verified in-scope estate enquiries | 12 | Distinct estate accounts with fit and decision-role review |
| Qualified demos | 6 | Completed demo record with source, role and need |
| Accepted trials | 3 | Signed scope and agreed activation date |
| Paid continuations business target | 1 | At least one paid continuation evidenced by payment and activation |

Trial close-out is a separate operational standard. Every accepted trial must have a documented outcome, including paid continuation, export and exit, deletion and exit, documented retention, extension, no response or not-fit reason. Complete close-out records do not satisfy the paid-continuation business target. Zero continuations means the business target was not met, even if every trial was closed correctly.

Count distinct estates, not form fills. Keep channel spend and support effort beside outcomes.

## Global go/no-go gate

The proposed direct, referral and organic launch target of 2026-10-01 requires owner approval and all of these:

- Product owner signs the public scope and technical wording.
- Privacy and security owner approves the public form, consent text, storage and trial data process.
- Implementation lead confirms the five-trial ceiling and the support owner. If the ceiling is not confirmed, remove it from public copy and set a lower approved number.
- Sales owner accepts enquiries and can meet the approved response SLA.
- Form or contact path, acknowledgement, failure path, CRM routing and suppression checks pass controlled tests in a non-production destination.
- UTM and event mapping join to one estate account without personal data in URLs or event payloads.
- Approved creative has claims, privacy, permission and product-truth checks.
- Owner approves any direct public campaign activation. This planning file does not give that approval.

Paid launch adds explicit owner approval for geography, total and per-channel caps, billing owner and account access. The 2026-10-05 earliest date is a proposed checkpoint, not current authority. End-to-end tracking QA must pass first. Budget remains TBD until continuation pricing, gross margin, implementation and support cost per trial, maximum acceptable CAC, maximum test loss and owner approval are documented.

Any missing minimum item is a no-go for the affected external action. Record the owner, deadline and evidence. Safe preparation can continue.
