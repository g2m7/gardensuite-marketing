## Purpose

Turn a relevant estate enquiry into a safe demo, a written trial and a documented day-30 decision. Keep one estate history across direct, referral, organic, Meta, Search and retargeting. Count verified estates and outcomes, not raw form fills.

Sarbani Associates owns the product conversation, setup, training and support. The sales owner and implementation lead must be named before the direct gate.

## Qualification before a demo

### Confirm the estate

- Canonical estate name, region, current client and active-sales status.
- Ownership and scale only as needed for service fit. Do not exclude an estate merely because it has older software.
- DNC, prior rejection, complaint and hard-bounce status.
- One account ID for every contact and source.

### Confirm the need and role

- A real attendance, hazira or office-review need is described.
- Contact role is owner, director, general manager, estate/garden manager or a relevant operational decision-maker.
- The person can approve or sponsor a trial decision.
- The estate understands the proposed GS Face field-to-office workflow.

### Confirm technical and privacy fit

- One approved Android device or a clear device question.
- Connectivity and field conditions are described without a promise.
- Worker-master source, office review and export owner are known.
- Privacy, security and data handling questions are recorded.
- No production face data is requested in the enquiry form or sales thread.

### Fit outcomes

| Result | Meaning | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified | Estate, role, need, device and privacy path can be assessed | Book demo or technical fit call |
| Nurture | Need exists but timing, scope or decision is unclear | Record next date and useful resource |
| Hold | Capacity, privacy, device or module issue needs a decision | Assign blocker owner and review date |
| Not fit | Product or service cannot support the request | Record reason respectfully and stop acquisition |
| Do not contact | Stop request, complaint or explicit DNC | Suppress all channels and retain only minimum stop evidence |

## Demo flow

Keep the first demo short and practical. Use fictional or permitted sample data.

1. **Agree the aim, 5 minutes:** Confirm the estate, role, attendance problem and what the viewer should learn.
2. **Show the field path, 10 minutes:** Face verification, hazira, section/work context and the approved device workflow. Do not claim an absolute result.
3. **Show the office path, 10 minutes:** Later sync, review, correction and one standard export, only where verified.
4. **Show the boundary, 5 minutes:** Proposed one-division or worker-group trial, up to 100 workers, one approved device, up to two users and remote setup. State exclusions and no auto-renewal.
5. **Show privacy, 5 minutes:** Explain the approved data process, no marketing-form face collection, and export, deletion or retention at trial end.
6. **Agree next step, 5 minutes:** Book a technical fit, send the written scope, hold with a date, or record not fit.

Offer `Book Free Demo` for a ready conversation, `Email Us` for a written question and `Request Puja Trial` for the public offer. Do not ask for a trial before the estate knows the exclusions and data agreement.

## Response SLA proposal

The internal proposal is:

- respond to a relevant enquiry within one working day;
- acknowledge a resource or privacy request on the same working day;
- assign a named sales owner and next action before the end of the working day;
- review a trial issue by severity and agreed support hours.

The sales owner must approve the wording, holiday cover and backup before it appears publicly. A response target is not a promise of a fix time. Record the actual response and resolution time separately.

## Demo handover to trial

### Before the agreement

- Product owner has verified the public build and allowed wording.
- Implementation lead has checked device, worker-group size, support and capacity.
- Privacy owner has approved notice, consent or approved basis, storage, access, export, deletion and retention.
- Sales owner has confirmed the estate is not current client, active negotiation, rejection or DNC.
- Campaign owner has confirmed the offer version and source.

### Trial agreement contents

1. Estate and division or worker group.
2. Maximum 100 workers, one approved Android device and up to two users, if the provisional scope is approved.
3. Agreed activation date and day-30 end date.
4. Free items: software access, remote setup, enrolment help, one training session, office review, one standard export and weekly review.
5. Exclusions: hardware, scales, travel, connectivity, data cleanup, custom integration and work outside scope.
6. No auto-renewal and no full ERP commitment.
7. Data roles, security, export, deletion or retention at trial end.
8. Support owner, response route and approved support hours.
9. Change-control path for extra workers, devices, users, modules or integrations.
10. Paid continuation choice and any separately quoted price.

### Acceptance record

The private tracker records `trial_id`, estate account, scope version, signature or written acceptance, activation date, end date, owners, data agreement, capacity decision and next review. The public docs contain no signature or PII.

## Onboarding runbook

### Before activation

- Confirm activation date, support owner, device, office reviewer, worker-master source and training attendee.
- Run the privacy/security gate. If it is not signed, use fictional data and do not collect production faces.
- Test the device, connectivity and fallback process. Record the result.
- Prepare a clean fictional or approved master and a recovery copy under the agreed access control.
- Send the estate a short start checklist and the agreed contact route.

### Activation day

- Verify the account, device, user roles and section/work context.
- Enrol only the agreed worker group. Do not ask the marketing team to handle worker face files.
- Run the first capture and review using an approved test or supervised record.
- Confirm the office user can find the record, see sync status, record a correction and export the agreed standard file.
- Log setup time, support time, issues, training attendance and first usable record.
- Send activation confirmation without including face data in email or chat.

### First-week support

- Review the first agreed records with the office owner.
- Check failed or pending sync states and the agreed fallback.
- Check worker and supervisor questions in plain language.
- Record changes separately from approved scope.
- Do not expand to a second division, device group or module without a new decision.

## Weekly trial review

Every active trial gets a review in the agreed week. The implementation lead owns the record; sales joins when there is a conversion or risk decision.

| Check | Question | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Use | Did the agreed group use the workflow as expected? | Counts and issue log, not a claim of accuracy |
| Sync | Did records reach the office when expected? | Device and sync status |
| Review | Did the office review and record corrections? | Review record and open issue |
| Enrolment | Were worker master and enrolment issues resolved? | Issue owner and date |
| Support | How many hours and what category? | Support log |
| Data | Is any personal or biometric data outside the approved process? | Privacy check |
| Scope | Did the estate request extra workers, devices, users or modules? | Change request |
| Decision | Continue, exit, extend or review on a named date? | Signed outcome and reason |

Do not set a numeric attendance-accuracy or savings target in public copy. Use the estate's own records and documented observations.

## Support and capacity

The provisional ceiling is five active trials. The implementation lead must confirm it before advertising. The owner must record:

- setup and training capacity per week;
- support hours and holiday cover;
- maximum worker-master load;
- device and connectivity exceptions;
- escalation and stop owner;
- what happens when a new trial would exceed the ceiling.

A paid campaign does not create an obligation to accept an unqualified trial. When capacity is full:

1. record the enquiry and fit result;
2. tell the estate the next review date or offer a waitlist only with permission;
3. do not promise activation without capacity;
4. pause or reduce paid spend if qualified demand exceeds the approved limit;
5. review whether the ceiling can change through a dated decision.

## Conversion decision

At day 30, the sales and implementation owners choose one path and record the reason:

- paid continuation with written scope, price, start date and owner;
- export and exit;
- deletion and exit;
- documented retention under the agreement;
- short extension with a reason, new date and capacity check;
- no response with the permitted follow-up count and close date;
- no fit because of device, data, capacity, module or service reason.

A trial does not auto-renew and does not become a full ERP. Do not use a deadline to pressure a worker, estate or support team. A paid decision must include hardware, travel, connectivity, custom work and modules as separate items when relevant.

## Attribution and account model

### Source values

Use the first source and latest meaningful source:

- `direct`
- `referral`
- `organic`
- `meta`
- `search`
- `retargeting`
- `existing_client_support`
- `other_approved`

Store `campaign_id`, `asset_id`, `keyword`, `cell_id` and `offer_version` in the private activity record. Do not put contact PII or worker data in URL parameters.

### Core attribution rule

1. The enquiry's account receives the first verified source.
2. Each later touch is an activity, not a new lead.
3. A conversion keeps first source for acquisition reporting and shows the assist path separately.
4. A referral gets the referrer relationship and the estate as a separate account.
5. A current client or support issue is not a net-new acquisition result.
6. A raw form fill, click or video view is not a verified estate.
7. If a source is unknown, record `unknown`; do not infer it from a later ad click.

## Campaign scorecard

Use [campaign-scorecard.csv](templates/campaign-scorecard.csv) as a header-only template. The private scorecard must be copied to a restricted workbook or approved CRM. Headings include:

`week/date`, `channel`, `campaign/asset id`, `currency`, `spend`, `enquiries`, `verified estate enquiries`, `demos`, `trials`, `paid continuations`, `lost reasons`, `reason_code`, `support hours`, `sales_hours`, `capacity_status`, `privacy_exceptions`, `decision_owner`, `decision_date`, `restart_stop_evidence` and `notes`.

For each week:

- count distinct estates at each stage;
- keep spend in INR and show zero when no spend, not blank or a forecast;
- show source and campaign ID;
- show a reason code and note for lost, no-response and no-conversion outcomes;
- show support hours separately from sales time;
- show capacity and privacy exceptions;
- never include names, phone numbers, email addresses, worker IDs or face data in the public plan.

### Core formulas

Use only when the denominator is non-zero:

- cost per verified estate = attributable spend / distinct verified estates;
- cost per completed demo = attributable spend / completed qualified demos;
- trial conversion = accepted trials / qualified demos, with the denominator stated;
- paid continuation rate = paid continuations / accepted trials, with the denominator stated;
- support load = recorded implementation and support hours / active trials.

If a denominator is zero, report `not yet measurable`. Do not report zero cost or a rate.

## Daily operating cadence

### Every working day during active acquisition

- Check inbound enquiries, resource requests, replies, DNC and privacy issues first.
- Apply suppression and stop requests before any queue or follow-up.
- Reconcile the form, acknowledgement, CRM, source and response owner.
- Review paid spend, broken events, platform warnings and lead quality if spend is active.
- Assign every relevant enquiry a next action and date.
- Check active-trial capacity and urgent support.
- Update the private scorecard and asset register.

### Weekly operating review

- Count distinct estates, qualified demos, accepted trials and paid continuations by source.
- Review support hours, activation blockers, scope changes and day-30 decisions.
- Review search terms, creative and landing path with paid owner.
- Review organic indexing, internal links, content and permission.
- Review referral, partner, LinkedIn and WhatsApp compliance.
- Decide capacity, spend, creative, page and follow-up actions.
- Record unresolved decisions with a named owner and deadline.

### Day 30 conversion review

- Send the agreed written continuation or exit notice.
- Confirm export, deletion or retention action.
- Record paid, exit, extension, no response or not-fit reason.
- Ask for a permitted case study only after the outcome is recorded; do not ask as a condition of support.
- Add objections and product questions to the asset and FAQ backlog.
- Do not count a paid continuation before payment and activation are evidenced.

## Internal goals

These are goals, not forecasts, and are reviewed by 2026-12-31:

- 12 verified in-scope estate enquiries;
- 6 qualified demos;
- 3 accepted trials;
- at least 1 paid continuation as the business target.

Every accepted trial must also have a documented operational outcome: paid continuation, export and exit, deletion and exit, documented retention, extension, no response or not-fit reason. These close-out records are a separate operational requirement. Zero paid continuations means the business target was not met, even if every trial was closed correctly.

A goal is not met by a click, a form fill, a raw reply or an unverified contact. Show the actual denominator and any capacity or privacy limit.

## Stop rules

Pause the sales or trial lane when:

- privacy, security or data handling is unresolved;
- a demo or sales claim differs from the approved product truth;
- a response owner or support owner is unavailable;
- a lead cannot be matched to one estate account;
- DNC, complaint, bounce or duplicate handling fails;
- a trial exceeds the approved scope or capacity;
- a worker or estate reports an unresolved data concern;
- the form or acknowledgement path loses a request;
- a prospective conversion is based on an unapproved price or unsupported result.

The stop owner records the trial or enquiry ID, time, issue, affected scope, action and restart evidence. A paused request stays paused until a named owner approves the next step.
