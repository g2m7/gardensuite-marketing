# Brevo Boundary

**Status:** Retired for cold outreach on 2026-08-05

Brevo is not the sending tool for the GardenSuite cold-email pilot.

Use [`SNOV_IO_CAMPAIGNS.md`](SNOV_IO_CAMPAIGNS.md) for the current 20-estate pilot.

Brevo may still be used for:

- website enquiries
- people who ask for GardenSuite information
- contacts who clearly opt in to later marketing
- customer or lead nurture that meets Brevo's permission rules

Do not use Brevo for:

- the first cold email
- automated cold follow-ups
- imported cold-contact lists
- the retired lists, templates, or automations

Official reference: [Brevo anti-spam policy](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/209405205-What-is-the-anti-spam-policy-of-Brevo)

No live Brevo list, automation, template, or contact was changed by this plan update.
