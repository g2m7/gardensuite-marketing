# Simple Outreach Plan

This is the working plan. Use the other documents only when more detail is needed.

## Goal

Get replies and attendance-demo requests from suitable tea estates.

## Who we contact

- Tea estates in West Bengal or Assam
- At least 50 hectares
- Not owned by a big corporate group
- One owner, director, general manager, estate manager, or garden manager per estate
- 20 estates in the first pilot

## What we send

Use one short tea-garden attendance sequence for everyone.

Personalisation is limited to:

- correct name, when verified
- correct estate name, when verified
- the real tea-garden attendance problem

Do not write a separate email for every estate. Do not invent a personal opening.

## Three emails

1. Day 0: face-verified attendance and proxy attendance
2. Day 5: offline field use and office review
3. Day 12: setup, training, support, and final question

Snov.io sends the sequence from the approved domain mailbox.

Stop immediately after a reply, unsubscribe, bounce, complaint, or manual stop.

## Marketing material

Do not attach a brochure, image, video, or long document to the first cold email.

The first email has one job: get a simple reply.

After a person replies or asks for details, send only the useful material:

- attendance brochure
- attendance product page
- short demo video
- demo booking details

The website can remain in the normal email signature.

## Before sending

- [ ] Choose the domain mailbox and named sender
- [ ] Fix SPF, DKIM, and DMARC
- [ ] Connect SMTP and IMAP to Snov.io
- [ ] Verify the 20 estate and email records
- [ ] Check the do-not-contact list
- [ ] Approve the three emails
- [ ] Test reply, unsubscribe, and bounce stopping
- [ ] Approve the final 20-contact batch

## One spreadsheet

Keep one row per estate with:

- estate name
- state
- hectares
- owner group
- contact name and role
- email and source
- current status
- last action
- next action
- do-not-contact status

## Simple statuses

Use only:

1. Research
2. Ready
3. Emailing
4. Replied
5. Demo
6. Closed

Keep `Do Not Contact` as a separate Yes or No field. It stops all outreach regardless of status.

## Pilot result

The pilot is useful if it produces:

- at least 2 relevant positive replies, or
- at least 1 qualified demo

Pause immediately for a spam complaint, suppression mistake, or hard bounce. Review the problem before continuing.

No external email may be sent without final approval from Sarbani Associates.
