# Simple Messaging and Channel Rules

## Main rule

Use one short email sequence for every approved role.

The message must be specific to tea gardens and attendance, but it does not need to be separately written for every estate.

## What personalisation means

Use only:

- the correct contact name, when verified
- the correct estate name, when verified
- the real tea-garden attendance problem

Do not invent personal facts. Do not add a weak opening line just to make the email look personal.

If the name is missing, use an approved neutral greeting.

## Three-email sequence

| Email | Day | Main point |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 | 0 | Face-verified attendance helps stop proxy attendance |
| 2 | 5 | Attendance works offline in the field and can be reviewed in the office |
| 3 | 12 | Sarbani Associates provides setup, training, and support; this is the final email |

Each email should:

- be plain text
- stay under about 120 words
- contain one main idea
- ask one easy question
- use simple tea-garden language
- identify GardenSuite and Sarbani Associates

Do not write `just checking in`.

## Marketing material

Marketing material is useful, but not in the first cold email.

Do not attach or embed:

- brochures
- images
- videos
- presentations
- long PDFs
- several links

The first email has one purpose: receive a simple reply.

Attachments and heavy HTML can make a cold email look like a bulk promotion, create security concern, distract from the question, and make delivery harder.

After someone replies or asks for more information, send the one item that answers their need:

- attendance brochure
- attendance product page
- short demo video
- demo booking details

The normal website link may remain in the sender signature.

## Claims

Allowed:

- helps stop proxy attendance
- face check before hazira
- works offline
- syncs when internet returns
- office review
- on-site setup and training
- built and supported by Sarbani Associates
- developed for tea gardens since 2000
- used by 20+ tea estates

Do not use:

- guaranteed
- no errors
- 100% automated
- zero paper
- stops proxy attendance completely
- unsupported savings or accuracy numbers

## Reply handling

| Reply | Action |
|---|---|
| Interested | Stop sequence and answer directly |
| Wants details | Send the relevant asset, not the entire marketing pack |
| Wants demo | Stop sequence and arrange the demo |
| Wrong person | Stop and verify any referred person |
| Not now | Stop and record a possible later date |
| No interest | Stop and close the account |
| Unsubscribe or stop | Stop and add to suppression immediately |
| Bounce | Stop and verify the address |

## Other channels

WhatsApp is used only after permission, an inbound enquiry, a positive email reply, or an approved existing relationship.

LinkedIn is not part of the first pilot.

## Approval checklist

- [ ] All three emails are approved
- [ ] Each email contains one new point
- [ ] Copy is specific to tea-garden attendance
- [ ] Name and estate fields have safe fallbacks
- [ ] No attachment or heavy HTML is present
- [ ] Only one question appears in each email
- [ ] Claims follow GardenSuite safety rules
- [ ] Final email clearly ends the sequence
- [ ] Stop and unsubscribe wording is clear
