# Simple Pipeline and Ownership

The pipeline belongs to the estate, not each email address.

## Six statuses

| Status | Meaning | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Research | Estate, size, ownership, or contact is being checked | Outreach owner |
| Ready | Estate and one contact meet all pilot rules | Outreach owner |
| Emailing | The Snov.io sequence has started | Outreach owner |
| Replied | A person replied and needs a human response | Outreach owner |
| Demo | A demo is being arranged or completed | Demo owner |
| Closed | Work is finished | Outreach or demo owner |

For `Closed`, record one outcome:

- Won
- Lost
- No Response

## Do Not Contact

Keep `Do Not Contact` as a separate Yes or No field.

When it is Yes:

- stop every scheduled email
- remove the contact from Snov.io
- add the email to the suppression register
- do not restart automatically

This field overrides every status.

## Ownership

Only two named owners are needed:

1. Outreach owner: research, data checks, Snov.io, replies, and spreadsheet updates
2. Demo owner: demos, evaluation, and commercial next steps

Each owner should have one backup.

## Reply rules

| Event | Action |
|---|---|
| Positive reply | Respond within 4 business hours |
| Demo request | Assign the demo owner |
| Wrong person | Stop and verify any referral |
| Not now | Stop and record a later date only if requested |
| No interest | Close as Lost |
| No reply after Email 3 | Close as No Response |
| Stop request | Set Do Not Contact to Yes immediately |
| Hard bounce | Stop and verify the remaining addresses |
| Spam complaint | Pause the whole pilot |

## Minimum handoff to demo owner

- estate name and location
- hectares and ownership check
- contact name and role
- contact's reply or question
- attendance problem, when known
- agreed next step

## Weekly review

Check only:

- Ready estates that have not started
- replies waiting for a response
- demos without a date
- closed estates without an outcome
- any Do Not Contact record still scheduled

All role names remain placeholders until Sarbani Associates assigns the actual people.
